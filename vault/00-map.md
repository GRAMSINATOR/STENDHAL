# Seven research territories — provisional exploration chart

The physical divisions are **expedition routes**, not a theory of what writing is. Any source may be linked from several routes. Cross-territory phenomena need not be physically duplicated.

| Route | Territory | Initial emphasis | Deliberate contradiction to seek |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Writers in the workshop | Practitioner accounts, letters, drafts, habits, aesthetic commitments, tacit craft; first deep excavation. | Process can be predesigned, discovered while writing, or moved between both; every writer's testimony is situated. |
| 02 | Microfabric of language | Phonology, syntax, punctuation, clause architecture, lexical texture, register, figurative composition. | A comma can be logical, rhythmic, dramatic, perceptual, cultural or several of these at once. |
| 03 | Narrative, subtext and worlds | Narrative time, viewpoint, polyphony, character, implicit meaning, imagery, form, interpretative instability. | Explicit explanation, withheld knowledge and resistant ambiguity can each be crafted, or can fail. |
| 04 | Rhetoric, speech and performance | Oratory, speechwriters, forensic and civic rhetoric, memorability, embodiment and spoken delivery. | Persuasive effect, ethical clarity, evidence and audience response may support or resist one another. |
| 05 | Science and technical knowledge | Scientific papers, evidence, mathematical exposition, causal qualification, technical communicative cultures. | Narrative intelligibility versus discipline-specific accuracy is an incomplete opposition. |
| 06 | Readers, cognition and evaluation | Empirical response, text worlds, foregrounding, processing, expertise, comprehension, interpretative plurality. | Measurable ease, remembered information, reader pleasure and literary quality are different constructs. |
| 07 | Failure, anti-formula and computational creation | Bad writing, critical disputes, AI-default patterns, evaluation limitations, creative algorithms, counterexamples. | Bad writing is real, but its diagnoses can be parochial, self-serving, or themselves formulaic. |

## Depth sequence

**1. Open terrain:** establish multiple incompatible conceptual entrances, especially primary authors and technical sources.
**2. Enter sources:** examine the actual text, authorial process, examples and counterexamples, with granular reference and source status.
**3. Polarise:** compare radically different models without forcing a reconciled midpoint.
**4. Dig underground:** study tactical operations, individual lexical/syntactic choices, multiple simultaneous roles, and interaction effects.
**5. Host adversaries:** seek grounded critiques from within the same tradition and from another discipline.
**6. Trial enaction:** demand that a candidate insight supports controlled generative practice, not just sophisticated commentary.
**7. Map absences:** note languages, eras, genres, schools, empirical populations and techniques not represented.

The route sequence is a *research management process*, never a universal composition procedure. Each territory can be expanded asymmetrically without requiring the others to conform.

## Acquisition priorities

Territory 01: first deep index and disagreements **created**.
Territories 02–07: grounded starting entries **seeded**; need their own heavy excavation passes.
The next tranche should deepen *one* territory using full texts, variants and manuscript examples rather than increase the bibliography alone.
