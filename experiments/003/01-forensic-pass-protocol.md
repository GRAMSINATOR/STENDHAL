# Experiment 003 — Perceptual overdrive / forensic pass

**Status:** protocol prepared, **not yet executed**. A first experiment must measure analytical *blind spots* and the ability to improve rather than merely reward long commentary.

## Aim

Compare a conventional one-pass literary/technical analysis to a painstakingly multi-pass system which can identify overlapping, nonlocal, nonstandard and hard-to-name features of a text.

This is an **analysis-first experiment**, not a proof of improved prose generation. Enactive testing follows once useful distinctions are found.

## Test material

Begin with two contrasting legally usable passages, each with context:
- one crafted passage with significant micro/macro interactions;
- one relatively plain scientific or explanatory paragraph.

Then stress the system with (a) experimental poetry, (b) intentionally mediocre prose, (c) STENDHAL Experiment 001's false-contradiction opening. Do not equate elevated style with high compositional sophistication.

## Independent annotation conditions

**Baseline:** one model pass given a standard, unconstrained request for close stylistic analysis.

**High-resolution procedure:** separate, independently seeded examinations by:
1. unlabelled noticing;
2. language material (sound, placement, syntax, punctuation, collocation, lexical fields);
3. semantic/inferential/pragmatic possibilities;
4. discourse and sequencing, including cross-paragraph carryover;
5. global/retroactive functions where sufficient broader context exists;
6. reader hypotheses and alternative interpretations;
7. adversarial counter-reading and negative tests.

After independent examinations, **revisit every local unit** with information acquired globally. Do not merely concatenate seven lists. Seek previously unseen cross-lens interactions and allow new categories.

## Annotation unit

Each observation can include a direct textual anchor, optional additional distant anchors, an explanation of the relation, alternative plausible interpretations, missing context, predicted implications if the feature changes, and *confidence in the observation*, not a numeric score of literary beauty.

The annotation form must allow **uncategorised notes** and a provisional "not yet represented" label. One span can have dozens of notes or none.

## Evaluation

Use expert human annotation where possible but do not pretend it establishes a final ground truth. Compare the **distinct defensible discoveries** produced by baseline and high-resolution conditions, the invalid discoveries rejected in adjudication, the number of genuinely new observations at each extra pass, missed cross-scale effects, and counterfactual predictions that hold on close examination.

Do not use a raw "techniques found" count as a quality score. Score substantive analytical coverage together with reliability and insight gained.

## Bridge to enaction

Choose a small number of **high-confidence, multi-function observations**. Ask the enactive system to construct a new passage exhibiting the corresponding **relationships**, not merely copying the surface device. Vary one aspect and inspect adjacent unintended changes.

**Failure case:** the system writes ornate AI prose that recites the analytical vocabulary or recreates the original structure thoughtlessly.

## Completion criterion

A credible first result is a documented set of mechanisms that ordinary analysis missed, a record of which new passes discovered them, and at least one previously missed property that becomes deliberately manipulable during enaction.

The experiment may also show the proposed procedure is too expensive or produces interpretive hallucinations. That is reason to revise the procedure, not declare success.
