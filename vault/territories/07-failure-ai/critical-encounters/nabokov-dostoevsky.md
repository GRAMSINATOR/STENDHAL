# Nabokov judges Dostoevsky

**Scholarly source:** Alexander Dolinin, *Nabokov, Dostoevsky, and Dostoevskyness*, *Russian Studies in Literature*, 1999. https://doi.org/10.2753/RSL1061-1975350442

**Reported remarks:** Dolinin documents Nabokov's recurring disdain toward Dostoevsky in lectures, interviews, letters and fiction. The targets include sentimentality, theatricality, crude humour, journalistic style, and the grounds on which his prestige rests.

**Source status:** scholarly report of primary positions; the original complete lecture or relevant letters were not consulted in this batch. We must not substitute Dolinin's explanatory frame for Nabokov's precise technical criticism.
