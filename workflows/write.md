# Workflow — Write an article

Use this for a new article from notes, reporting, source material or a research question.

## Stage 0 — Intake

Resolve:
- target reader;
- article format;
- requested style/profile;
- desired length;
- publication constraints;
- whether external research is allowed/required.

Do not ask for information already present in supplied sources.

## Stage 1 — Evidence map

Read `brain/02-evidence-and-justification.md`.

Build the claim ledger. Separate known facts from attributed claims, inference and unknowns.

Research blockers before prose.

## Stage 2 — Proposition

Read `brain/01-editorial-proposition.md`.

Write:
- editorial proposition;
- story question;
- reader prior model;
- model change;
- payoff.

If the proposition is weak, keep reporting or reframe.

## Stage 3 — Choose format

Pick one primary format from `formats/`.

The format answers: what kind of reading experience best fits the evidence?

Do not pick a feature merely because the requested publication often runs features.

## Stage 4 — Build the beat map

Read `brain/03-structure-and-sequencing.md`.

Outline 3–7 major beats. For each:
- editorial job;
- new information;
- evidence;
- reason it appears here;
- handoff to next beat.

Choose an opening mechanism and an ending function.

## Stage 5 — Load style

Read `brain/07-style-engine.md` and the requested profile in `styles/`.

Translate the profile into decisions about:
- sequencing;
- compression;
- scene;
- authorial presence;
- judgement;
- rhythm;
- lexical markedness.

Do not imitate at sentence level yet.

## Stage 6 — Draft

Draft from evidence and beat map.

While drafting:
- keep claims in their correct evidential state;
- let paragraph lengths follow function;
- allow plain language to dominate;
- use marked phrases sparingly;
- do not explain every implication immediately;
- do not force a conclusion at every paragraph end.

## Stage 7 — Structural edit

Run `editors/structural-editor.md`.

Fix order before line style.

## Stage 8 — Evidence edit

Run `editors/evidence-editor.md`.

Resolve blockers before polishing.

## Stage 9 — Line / voice edit

Read:
- `brain/04-sentence-paragraph-rhythm.md`
- `brain/05-voice-and-language.md`
- `editors/line-and-rhythm.md`

Tune cadence and diction to the selected profile.

## Stage 10 — Adversarial anti-slop pass

Run:
- `brain/06-anti-slop.md`
- `editors/slop-and-comprehension.md`

Diagnose synthetic texture and comprehension separately.

## Stage 11 — Final verification

Check:
- proposition developed rather than repeated;
- key facts sourced;
- uncertainty visible;
- headline/standfirst fit article;
- opening earns attention without deception;
- ending adds rather than summarizes;
- style profile is detectable through decisions, not costume.

Return clean prose unless process notes were requested.
