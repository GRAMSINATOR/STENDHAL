# EXP-017 — Two source-derived capabilities, three small targets, overlapping perceptions

**2026-10-13.** A deliberate methodological reduction following operator criticism of EXP-016's overlong, over-performed literary prose. **Status:** source-close theory testing + in-context illustrative writing probes; no independently rated improvement and no autonomous critic model.

## Research question

Can a writing system build **detailed and even redundant perceptions**, preserve mutually illuminating or competing interpretations, and nonetheless make **very few, high-leverage expressive decisions** rather than putting every identified technique onto the page? Can it recognise when to apply a source-specific method, and when to **leave a text alone**?

The operator's 2026-10-13 refinement: talented writing often has strongly blended relationships; apparent simplicity need not imply simple craft. Many techniques may become inseparable in the finished utterance, and several lenses can describe a single effect. Sometimes deliberately foregrounded devices, repeated insistence or conspicuous tonal collision are also correct for the work. No universal mandate for brevity, invisibility or “tastefulness” is inferred.

## Readable outcomes

- [01 — Austen / Giono source-specific cards](01-source-cards.md): two restricted methods with provenance, what was actually observed, failure and non-use conditions. **Not literary personalities to impersonate.**
- [02 — actual original microtrials](02-actual-microtrials.md): A = socially consequential warning, B = greenhouse warning light, C = ordinary swimming-pool wristband. Each contrasts raw text, plain polish and where warranted a narrowly selected intervention; **C preserves its baseline**. No aesthetic winner asserted.
- [03 — cross-check with other writers and reader research](03-convergent-perception-and-restraint.md): Proust/Hemingway/Poe/Woolf/Austen, reading experiments on free indirect discourse and foregrounding, a contemporary report of Stein's repetition/insistence view. Explicitly keeps disagreements and limitations.

## What changed from EXP-016

1. **Less prose, more exact observation:** around 35–50 words rather than 500-word scenes.
2. **Source style is an optional concentrated local choice**, not a command to emit figures of speech.
3. **Multiple perceptual descriptions can converge on the same source span.** Do not count each as a separate independent proof of excellence.
4. **Avoid fabrication of psychological wisdom** by forcing significance onto simple objects.
5. **A branch can do nothing.** For a short pool passage, no Austen/Giono intervention has a good source-backed reason.
6. **Strong and visibly repetitive writing remains permitted.** Minimalism is not the standard; an aesthetically striking device may be the purpose.
7. **Raw baseline is a legitimate contender.** The researcher has not proven that source-selected writing wins.

## Next credible validation

Use the same compact source profiles but give an independent perceiver a **witness-only** text with no label of the intended method. Ask what effects it can actually support, with counterreadings. Then give an editor two or three possible changes plus no edit. Record *which choice* was made before looking at the alternative variants. Have human readers or independently solicited critics compare blind, including cases where no edit wins.

**Do not** convert these provisional source profiles into a new universal writing taxonomy or claim the single-context microtexts prove anything about LLM style quality. Future additions should include an intentionally verbose/insistent source, not only methods that permit compact lines.

## Operator-selected active training profile (2026-10-13)

After the cross-source qualification, the operator clarifies that **for training now**, restraint is preferable because sophisticated maximalism requires more reliably developed craft than our first prototypes exhibit. Use [TR01 — restraint-first](../../design/training-regimes/TR01-restraint-first.md): hold one radical governing choice, generate **one to four sentences** by default, let lexical and sonic fields, context and implication do multiple jobs, avoid grand pseudo-insights and unnecessary re-explanation, and keep the unmodified baseline in contention. Broad and redundant perception is welcome; conspicuous separate enactment of every perceived technique is not. This is **a phase-limited pragmatic preference**, not a repeal of Stein, Woolf, Poe or the evidence that deliberate excess can be brilliant.

These are **future rehearsal instructions**. EXP-017's existing micro-passages were authored before this explicit training regime and do not verify that TR01 improves them.
