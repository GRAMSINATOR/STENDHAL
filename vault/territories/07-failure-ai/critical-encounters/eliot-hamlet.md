# T. S. Eliot on Hamlet

**Original essay:** *Hamlet and His Problems* (1919; The Sacred Wood, 1920). https://teachers.americanliterature.com/author/ts-eliot/essay/hamlet-and-his-problems

**Direct textual study:** Eliot calls *Hamlet* an artistic failure, adducing inherited dramatic material, excess or inconsistent scenes, varying verse, and the problem of emotion unsupported by an adequate dramatic situation. He questions Goethe and Coleridge's interpretations for projecting their own concerns onto the character.

**Source status:** the essay itself was inspected. Eliot also acknowledges the play's great interest. The historical reconstruction and account of Shakespeare's internal artistic difficulty remain Eliot's critical claims, not established authorial facts.

**Further study:** compare Eliot's named scenes and verse passages directly against the play and later expert responses. Posterity is no substitute for examining his argument.
