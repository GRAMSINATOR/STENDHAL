# Territory 01 — Polarities, incompatible assumptions and investigative contrasts

These are **analytic contrasts** unless an actual expressed disagreement is documented. Avoid declaring authors opponents merely because their habits differ.

## A. Is form conceived before words, or discovered through them?

- **Nabokov W01:** described a prefigured pattern and nonlinear card-based composition.
- **Smith W02:** describes herself as a micro-manager; contrasts the advance-planner.
- **Didion W07:** describes grammar and mental imagery as reciprocally informative.
- **Catteau/Dostoevsky W04:** manuscript reconstruction shows plans mutable during drafting.

**Tactical question:** can our enactive harness accommodate planning-before-realisation and discovery-through-realisation, including hybrids, without privileging either?

**Bad inference:** "good writers always plan" or "planning kills creativity".

## B. What does the sentence unit mean?

- **Klinkenborg W13:** treat individual sentence construction as a primary site of thought and deliberate revision.
- **Tufte W12 / Fish W17:** structure can be elaborate, relational, and capable of extended effects.
- **Prose W11:** highly granular reading traverses words → sentences → paragraphs → narration, not one-function units.
- **Didion W07:** arrangement changes the represented object and is changed by it.

**Tactical question:** what material transformations move meaning, while keeping a phrase's distant functions visible?

**Bad inference:** "short is clear" or "long is sophisticated".

## C. Is explanation the fundamental virtue?

- **Orwell W16:** concerns about degraded thought via abstraction, cliché and euphemism.
- **Stein W14:** recursive formal experimentation resists propositional summary.
- **Morrison W15:** parable and ethical interrogation join performance and content.
- **Duras W09:** writing as experience and meditative discovery.

**Tactical question:** when is declaring the central proposition necessary, merely uninteresting, or actively destructive?

**Bad inference:** treating ambiguity as incompetence, or opacity as genius.

## D. Is style a learned repertoire, an enacted experiment, or an identity?

- **Le Guin W06:** explicit exercises train control.
- **Queneau W10:** same narrative material becomes an experimental basis for many forms.
- **Bukowski W05:** distinctive identity and judgments documented in letters, but individual mechanisms need close reading.
- **Hemingway W08:** another practitioner world; don't flatten to short sentences.

**Tactical question:** how does a trained operation enter a distinct author's world without becoming a superficial costume?

## E. Does authorial testimony describe causation?

An interview may contain performance, retrospective rationalisation, real observation, contradiction or aspirational self-image. Compare with notebooks, drafts, letters and controlled transformations.

**Tactical question:** what can we verify from text and draft changes that self-report cannot establish?

## Initial refusal set

Reject polarities based solely on macho/dainty, classical/modern, high/low, short/long, clear/obscure, popular/literary or rule-breaking/rule-following. These erase richer differences in *process and structure*. Preserve such categories only when an actual source makes them analytically useful.
