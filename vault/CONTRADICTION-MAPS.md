# Qualified contradictions as generators of craft research

**Status:** working acquisition method. Not a universal aesthetics, not an instruction to force every source into a binary, and not the final architecture of STENDHAL.

## Research thesis

A **high-level, well-qualified contradiction** can be a generative map of distinct assumptions, representations, priorities, microtechniques, multi-scale operations, failure modes and creative procedures.

A contrast is most valuable not when it leads to an easy verdict or midpoint, but when investigating it **branches into actionable worlds of craft**. Each side may generate multiple subfamilies. Differences can also reveal a third unexpected procedure that cannot be obtained by averaging their obvious traits.

**The disagreement isn't the result. Its technical consequences are the result.**

## Qualification gate — avoid inventing false oppositions

STENDHAL's first writing experiment manufactured a "contradiction" from independent library metrics. The vault must not reproduce that error at a scholarly level.

Before treating a contrast as a high-level contradiction, establish:

- **Two grounded positions.** Attribute specific claims to actual sources, with wording, date, edition, context and confidence. A scholar's reconstruction and an author's testimony are different evidence.
- **A shared question.** Do the positions actually make competing demands **about the same decision**, at a comparable level of abstraction?
- **Scope.** Under which audience, medium, scale, writing regime, stage or goal do they oppose one another?
- **Type of divergence.** Logical incompatibility, procedural incompatibility at a declared time, competing value systems, different descriptions of the same activity, incommensurable aims, or only superficially contrasting habits?
- **Counterexamples and coexistence.** If both claims can be true under some conditions, say so. The contradiction may still be productive as a *conditional choice*, but do not pretend it is a universal binary.
- **Undecidable remainder.** What part of the positions is unexplained, under-sourced or still open to interpretation?

If the claims are not meaningfully opposed, demote them to **productive juxtaposition** or **compatibility study**. Neither needs rhetorical drama to be valuable.

## How to excavate a qualified contradiction

For **each position**, and then for their interactions:

1. **Ontology:** What entities, relationships, reader assumptions and forms of meaning does it recognise? What does it exclude?
2. **Generating process:** What is fixed beforehand, what is open, when may objectives change, how is discovery allowed to happen?
3. **Perceptual demands:** Which details must be seen (word, syntax, rhythm, motif, context, reader time), including those conventional labels miss?
4. **Tactical fabric:** Identify manipulations of diction, punctuation, syntax, emphasis, recursion, reveal order, juxtaposition and other micro- and meso-operations, without assigning a sentence one job.
5. **Cross-scale coupling:** What changes locally when a global feature is fixed or freed? What local discoveries cause global restructuring? Track interactions rather than merely listing devices.
6. **Enactive machinery:** What explicit procedures or representations are required to enact this world intentionally? What would a constrained realiser be allowed to decide?
7. **Failure and bad writing:** What failures are characteristic *inside the world*? What would an external critic wrongly condemn? What genuine weakness might its advocates overlook?
8. **Boundary and hybrid possibilities:** Under what conditions can the two operate in parallel, sequentially, antagonistically or not at all? Never assume blending is neutral or desirable.
9. **Tests:** Counterfactual rewrites, new-source transfer, intentional perturbations, adverse-condition trials and human criticism. Document preservation losses.
10. **Residual unknowns:** Name important mechanism candidates not captured by existing schemas, not just "future work" platitudes.

These questions are an excavation lens, **not a compulsory writing workflow**. Different territories may require different operational instruments.

## A polarity map is a branching graph

```text
grounded divergence (qualified)
    ├─ model/world A
    │   ├─ generating process A
    │   ├─ unique / shared tactical families
    │   ├─ perceptual needs + scale couplings
    │   ├─ characteristic breakdowns
    │   └─ new questions
    ├─ model/world B
    │   ├─ generating process B
    │   ├─ unique / shared tactical families
    │   ├─ perceptual needs + scale couplings
    │   ├─ characteristic breakdowns
    │   └─ new questions
    ├─ conditional interfaces / incompatibilities
    ├─ possible third regimes / transformations
    └─ experiments that may reject the original contrast
```

A single pole may itself contain **internal contradictions**, rather than being treated as a coherent monolith.

## Relationship to the vault and the future engine

- A *territory* organises research expeditions.
- A *qualified contradiction* is a cross-source **discovery and mapping instrument**.
- A *technique or process* is a candidate enactive capability, not automatically licensed by being discussed in a source.
- A *profile or world* may own unique process rules and override ordinary composition logic.
- The future *atlas* may map conditional usefulness, failure and intercompatibility, but must not impose universal ratings.
- The *research ledger* retains unadopted models, minority interpretations and unresolved evidence.

## Success criterion

The map succeeds only if it produces **new, specific, defeasible understanding**: explanatory distinctions or controlled operations not available from reading each source independently. It fails if it generates two generic lists of adjectives, universalises a contingent method, invents a conflict, or collapses the contrast into facile "balance."
