# EXP-017 / 03 — Convergent perception and selective craft: source stress test

**2026-10-13.** Operator hypothesis: a sophisticated passage can support many overlapping analyses while making only a few radical, consistent expressive choices. Distinct craft effects can blend so well that individual techniques become hard to isolate. A broadly perceptive writer need not visibly perform everything they perceive.

## A distinction that must survive

**Perceptual redundancy ≠ output redundancy.** One precise phrase can be examined as temporal ordering, epistemic status, social consequence, focal access, rhythm and syntax. Multiple lenses can describe the **same** relationship, however, so their agreement is not automatically independent evidence for literary quality. Some lenses may find nothing; no model should invent an effect to fill a slot. A highly conspicuous device can also be excellent art.

## Source-supported and competing positions

- **Proust — style as vision, not a detachable technique inventory.** His *Le Temps retrouvé* describes an artist's particular perception of the world as constitutive of style. Original-language facsimile witness: https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Proust_-_Le_Temps_retrouv%C3%A9,_1927,_tome_2.djvu/45 . **Supports** a coherent perceptual orientation behind many decisions; **does not prove** that annotating more creates such an orientation.
- **Hemingway — knowledgeable omission.** In *Death in the Afternoon* the iceberg principle distinguishes meaningful omissions supported by understanding from omission out of ignorance. Source-aware reproduction: https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2015/09/14/omission . **Supports** the asymmetry between latent knowledge and explicit expression; **warns** against blank, under-supported minimalism.
- **Poe — consciously unified effect.** In the primary Hawthorne review, Poe argues that multiple choices should contribute, directly or indirectly, to a deliberately planned effect. https://www.eapoe.org/WorkS/prescott/fcp09c09.htm . **Supports** convergence and economy; **conflicts** with treating emergent/revised intention as equally fundamental.
- **Woolf — form responsive to heterogeneous experience.** *Modern Fiction* objects to compulsory conventional event structure and describes shifting and apparently disconnected impressions. https://americanliterature.com/author/virginia-woolf/book/the-common-reader/modern-fiction . **Counterposition to rigid Poe-like orchestration:** convergence can be discovered during writing and need not reduce to one predecided payoff.
- **Austen/free indirect discourse — co-present voices in one utterance.** Daniel Gunn's scholarly abstract explicitly describes narrator/character mixture; Rachel Provenzano Oberman studies its meaning for *Emma*, and Joe Bray's reading experiment suggests subsequent context can affect POV recognition. https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61883/chapter-abstract/547772880 ; https://online.ucpress.edu/ncl/article-abstract/64/1/1/67583/Fused-Voices-Narrated-Monologue-in-Jane-Austen-s ; https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0963947007072844 . **Supports** dense coexistence with attribution sometimes undecidable.
- **Miall and Kuiken — foregrounding has measurable effects.** Four studies reported slower reading and higher strikingness/affect ratings at foregrounded passages. Abstract, not the full methodology, inspected: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0304422X94000115 . **Disconfirms seamlessness as a universal aim:** an intrusive, visible device may be the expressive point.
- **Gertrude Stein — repetition as insistence.** A 1934 lecture was reported as distinguishing apparent repetition from expressive insistence; this is a contemporary newspaper report, not a collated published lecture: https://aadl.org/aa_news_19341215_p1-miss_stein_states . **Disconfirms** automatic de-duplication as a universal editing principle.

## A concrete overlapping-lens perception

From [EXP-017 microtrials](02-actual-microtrials.md):

> At four the system failed. Mara reread the junior engineer's note, sent Tuesday. She had dismissed him in front of the team. She went into the stairwell, the note still open on her phone.

Several lenses legitimately converge: (1) Tuesday places her knowledge before failure; (2) the public dismissal creates social consequences; (3) rereading the note implies an interpretive adjustment without asserting shame; (4) retaining the open note after withdrawal gives the object a second position in reader-time; (5) the stairwell offers bodily separation from the team. **These are not five separate techniques needing five lines of explanation, nor five independent votes proving the passage excellent.** They are overlapping descriptions of the same simple situation. No strong claim is made about local phonological achievement.

The enactment itself can therefore remain minimal: **do not add a sentence saying she felt ashamed**. Or choose the shorter, explicit [A1] version if the task requires efficiency. The relevant creative decision is not the count of lenses activated.

Second example: the greenhouse's green light changes possible meaning after an alarm-disconnection disclosure. Temporal, causal, tonal and inferential readings can converge on one repeated object. But the exact electronics of the green indicator are unspecified, so the perceptual system must not claim a fully verified mechanical contradiction. The repetition could be effective or over-insistent.

Third example: the abandoned wristband at the pool. If neither the Austen nor Giono profile reveals a supported further action, **do not modify the text**. That is a positive application of knowledge: a grounded abstention, not an absent capability.

## Training / evaluation proposals (unrun)

1. Independently give critics the **same short textual witness** and several incompatible lenses. Track anchored observations and genuine disagreement, without summing lens labels into a prose-quality score.
2. Compare direct baseline, one selected source-grounded intervention, forced use of all perceived devices, and **no edit**. Blind readers should assess the local experience, not the generator's declared reasoning.
3. Test both precise restrained prose and intensely conspicuous/insistent prose so the methodology cannot win by calling sparse writing tasteful.
4. Reject confident synergies that rest on absent textual witnesses or invented reader response; preserve the possibility that several lens observations describe one causal relation.
5. Ask whether broader perception improves **selection**, not whether it automatically reduces word count or increases craft complexity.

**Status:** source-based triangulation and a same-context illustrative reading. No independent reader evaluation and no finding that “more perception” causally improves restraint. The principle is a researchable asymmetry, not a new universal stylistic prescription.
