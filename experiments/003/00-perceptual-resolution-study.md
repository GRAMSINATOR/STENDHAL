# Experiment 003 — Perceptual resolution before compositional ontology

**State:** proposed experiment, not yet run or scored. Designed following the operator's rejection of one-job sentence models and premature vector taxonomies.

## Objective

Determine which analytical representations uncover **distinct, defensible, interacting mechanisms** in short written passages, and which distinctions actually help a generative system exercise better control. We are trying to discover the shape of useful invariances, not confirm a proposed schema.

## Specimens and provenance

Select short, legally usable excerpts representing **at least**:
- a highly crafted literary passage with nonlocal implications;
- a quiet/ordinary but effective passage;
- a deliberately unusual poetic or experimental passage;
- a scientific/technical paragraph with its own density and discourse rules;
- a flawed synthetic passage, including Experiment 001's false-contradiction opening.

Keep contextual neighbours and record the excerpt's location in the larger work. The same isolated line may support very different interpretations when placed back in context. Use author-permitted or public-domain material where redistribution is necessary; do not clone living writers.

## Multiple competing readings (do not supply the 'techniques' in advance)

Run separate analyses that **do not see each other's annotation**. Prompts should solicit concrete evidence, counterexamples, and uncertainty, and should allow 'no convincing device here'.

A. **Micro-linguistic**: phonology where relevant, syntax, punctuation, lexical texture, position, presupposition and register.
B. **Compositional**: relations among units, preparation and delayed return, changing information or images, thematic/semantic pressure, patterns and breaks.
C. **Reader-model**: hypotheses about knowledge, mental worlds, inferences, attention and possible affect; acknowledge plausible disagreement.
D. **Situated/regime**: authorial world, genre, intent if actually evidenced, and alternatives that would be valid in another regime.
E. **Adversarial**: challenge other readings, diagnose post-hoc projection, invalid logic, false depth and inflated technique counts.

These are **trial perspectives**, not eternal namespaces.

## Compare resolutions

1. **Close**: inspect one adjective/verb/comma and possible relations to the surrounding sentence.
2. **Middle**: inspect sentences and paragraphs as overlapping sequences; map carried-over qualifications, rhythmic events and implicit handoffs.
3. **Distant**: inspect motifs, referential echoes, reader-time trajectories and structural consequences across passages.
4. **Reverse**: begin with a global/reader effect and trace several plausible, nonexclusive contributing mechanisms downward.

Cross-layer mappings may be many-to-many and nonlocal; do not assign one 'job' per unit.

## Counterfactual intervention tests

For selected observations, create controlled alternatives:
- change a supposedly important adjective; observe semantic, rhythmic, focalization, metaphorical and distant-motif consequences;
- relocate or delete a punctuation mark; analyse changed reading paths;
- remove a lexical field's explicit anchor while preserving its distributed associations;
- reverse the order of two passages; inspect what changes in anticipation and retrospective meaning;
- perturb a local feature that should **not** alter the global construction: negative control;
- change a rule of composition, not merely a parameter, and see whether a new regime emerges.

Avoid attributing changes to one factor if multiple variables moved. Some interactions will not be isolatable.

## Two notions of invariance to seek

**Representational invariance:** which explanatory relationships remain identifiable across multiple legitimate decompositions of the same passage?

**Enactive invariance:** which intended functions or constraints can survive change of wording, syntax, perspective, order or register, within a given regime?

Do **not** postulate an invariant definition of quality or universal 'best technique'.

## Evaluation

Capture specific claims and textual spans, overlap/disagreement among raters, confidence and alternatives, changes induced by controlled perturbations, and whether one representation yields **more controllable writing** than another.

Failure can be informative: an alleged technique may disappear when context returns; a device may be visible but causally inert; two analyses may be simultaneously valid but incompatible as writing procedures.

A good result is not 'we found 100 devices'. It is 'we identified mechanisms that survived adversarial scrutiny and enabled distinct, precise interventions without degrading adjacent values'.

## Immediate architecture implication

Do **not** promote the old `experiments/002/composition-field.json` into the canon. It is one **deliberately incomplete representation candidate**. Experiment 003 must be permitted to recommend a different substrate, a hybrid of multiple representations, or something not graph/vector-like at all.
