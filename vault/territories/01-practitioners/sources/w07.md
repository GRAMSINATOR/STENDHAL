## What the source establishes or invites us to study

Argues from her own practice that sentence arrangement is inseparable from how a mental image becomes understood; syntax itself changes meaning.

## Tentative tactical extraction — to test, not canonise

Model reciprocal operations between envisaged image and syntax; changing words may expose previously unknown properties of the image.

## Polarities, tensions and countercases

An explicit challenge to 'content first, surface realisation last' as a universal pipeline.
