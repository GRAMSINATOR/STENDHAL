# GR06 — Distinguish draft differences, reconstructed sequences and inner processes

**Companion to** [GR05 actual rough folios](GR05-flaubert-rough-draft-variants.md).

A page full of corrections is stronger evidence about **observable writing operations** than a finished paragraph, but it does **not** let us inspect what a writer consciously, let alone unconsciously, considered.

## Five progressively stronger propositions, each requiring new evidence

1. **Witness difference:** two documented manuscript/print states have a different noun or clause. Proven by source alignment.
2. **Authorial hand:** the change is made in a manuscript attributed to the writer. Needs manuscript provenance/palaeography.
3. **Temporal sequence:** one change preceded another. Requires reliable draft dating/overwriting/ink or paper evidence; archival order is not enough.
4. **Reasons and design:** the author changed a word to achieve a chosen perceptual effect. Requires letters, notebooks, explicit testimony or cautious inference; manuscript alone does not prove the motive.
5. **Reader effect:** a change actually alters a population's perceived texture, feeling or inference. Requires direct readers, listening, or justified external methods.

Do not promote 1 directly to 4 or 5.

## Evidence we now have

From the *L'Éducation sentimentale* opening archive:
- Rough drafts, autograph, printed witnesses with precise source links.
- Observed deletion of a complete boarding vignette and multiple lexical/syntactic alternatives.
- A likely useful acoustic contrast in the documented variant nouns *sifflement / bruissement*.
- A richer distinction in their surrounding expression, *disparaissait sous / s'absorbait dans*.

## Evidence we do NOT have

- A secured chronological ordering of folios 599_001r, 002r, 003r and 005r.
- Flaubert's stated reasoning for these particular variants.
- Original handwritten page individually re-transcribed here.
- Measured auditory reception.
- A claim that more revisions necessarily imply better art.

## Relationship to 'recompose after erasure'

The presence of several drafts and their changed material does not prove the author completely erased a previous text and re-created it **without looking**. That is a separate practitioner/process hypothesis recorded in [GR04](GR04-rewrite-without-looking.md). Do not use the Flaubert archive as spurious experimental verification of GR04.

## Future source triangulation

Photographic folios + specialist diplomatic transcriptions + copyists' text + public print witness + author's contextual correspondence, kept separately labelled. That research is not a universal writing method; it is a way to observe what actually happened.
