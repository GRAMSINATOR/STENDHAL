# Brief F2 — comic first-person fiction

**Venue and reader:** a humour and fiction anthology. Readers who want energy and comic momentum.

**Task:** write a piece of **300–400 words** in a high-energy, comic first-person voice. Exaggeration and digression are welcome if they keep moving.

**Fixed situation:**

- The narrator is a wedding DJ.
- During the evening reception, the power fails halfway through a song the father of the bride specifically requested. Do not name the song or quote lyrics.
- There is a generator in the car park. It belongs to the catering van.
- The bride's grandmother is present.
- The piece covers the time from the power cut to the moment music resumes, by whatever means.

**Open:** everything else, including how the music resumes.
