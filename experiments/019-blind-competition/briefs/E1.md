# Brief E1 — opinion column

**Venue and reader:** the work-life column of a general-interest newspaper. Readers with office jobs, many of them sceptical of management advice.

**Task:** write an opinion piece of **450–600 words** on the question: **Should workplaces refuse to hold any meeting without a written agenda?** Include a headline.

**Constraints:**

- Take a clear position, either for or against.
- Engage seriously with the strongest argument on the other side.
- Do not cite statistics, studies, surveys or named experts. Argue from reasoning and ordinary working experience.
