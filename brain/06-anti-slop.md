# 06 — Anti-slop

Anti-slop is diagnosis, not superstition.

Do not "humanize" by banning a handful of words and adding random imperfections. Synthetic prose is usually visible in repeated structures, equal weighting, predictable transitions and frictionless over-explanation.

## Structural tells

Watch for density of these patterns:

- every paragraph performs the same amount of work;
- every assertion is immediately explained;
- ideas repeatedly arrive in threes;
- each paragraph closes with a little conclusion;
- the thesis is restated instead of developed;
- consecutive paragraphs use identical shapes;
- discourse markers appear mechanically at paragraph openings without adding logical information;
- all sentence lengths converge toward the same middle;
- rhetorical questions occur on schedule;
- oppositions recur as "not X but Y";
- the article gives equal space to unequal points;
- headings or bold-label bullets substitute for prose.

One instance may be fine. Density is the signal.

## Connectives are not slop

Words such as `however`, `therefore`, `yet`, `still`, `indeed`, `instead`, `more importantly` and `in other words` are legitimate rhetorical tools.

Flag them only when:
- the logical relation they claim is absent;
- the same marker recurs mechanically;
- several consecutive paragraphs depend on explicit signposting;
- the marker substitutes for an actual transition in thought.

Do not delete visible rhetoric merely to look less machine-made.

See `brain/05a-cohesion-and-connectives.md`.

## Lexical tells

Treat these as warning lights, not automatic crimes:
- delve;
- leverage as a vague verb;
- robust without a measurable property;
- landscape as a generic field;
- navigate the complexities of;
- unlock;
- seamless;
- transformative;
- empower;
- at its core;
- it is important to note;
- in today's rapidly evolving;
- this isn't just X, it's Y;
- the result? X.

A necessary word remains necessary. The problem is stock function, not spelling.

## Abstraction pass

Search for nouns such as:
complexity, efficiency, burden, solution, process, capability, value, transformation, innovation.

For each, ask whether a person, object, action, number or decision can carry the meaning instead.

## Rhythm pass

Look only at shapes:
- sentence lengths;
- paragraph lengths;
- punctuation;
- openings;
- endings.

Do not fix monotony by random fragmentation. Change the underlying function.

## Repetition pass

Search for repeated rhetorical moulds:
- triads;
- em-dash asides;
- "This means";
- "In other words";
- "The result";
- "From X to Y";
- negation reversals;
- one-sentence paragraph beats;
- repeated paragraph-opening connectives.

Keep the best instance. Remove most of the rest.

## Quirk pass

Highlight every:
- unusual adjective;
- metaphor;
- coinage;
- joke;
- striking analogy.

Delete at least half unless the piece is intentionally voice-forward. The survivors must name, reveal or pace something.

## Comprehension is separate

A draft can avoid AI texture and still be unreadable.

Check:
- buried lede;
- missing throughline;
- undefined acronyms;
- named-entity overload;
- statistics without comparison;
- unexplained specialist terms;
- dense paragraphs;
- telegraphic colon-label prose;
- missing logical transitions.

Do not sacrifice comprehension merely to look less machine-made.

## Dead-fish test

Remove publication name, typography and byline.

Read three consecutive paragraphs.

Can you detect real decisions in:
- scale;
- pace;
- specificity;
- information order;
- attitude?

If not, the prose is dressed rather than alive.
