# Orwell reads Tolstoy on Shakespeare

**Primary essay:** George Orwell, *Lear, Tolstoy and the Fool* (1947): https://orwell.ru/library/essays/lear/english/e_ltf

**What he says:** Orwell criticises Tolstoy's hostile paraphrase of *King Lear*, points to poetry and the Fool as elements insufficiently perceived in Tolstoy's assessment, and notes features of the work that may survive verbal paraphrase. Importantly, Orwell **also agrees** that the play is structurally sprawling and contains improbabilities.

**Source status:** primary full essay examined. Orwell's suggestion about Tolstoy's personal reasons for disliking the work is a psychological interpretation, not a demonstrated fact. His survival-of-the-work criterion is his evaluative claim, not this vault's rule.
