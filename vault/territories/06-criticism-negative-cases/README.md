# Region 06 — Great critics, great targets, negative prose evidence

**Initial scouting directive, 2026-10-12.**

A negative judgment of a celebrated writer is a particularly valuable **qualified critical observation**, but a critic's fame is not itself proof of the judgment. Prioritise exact published reviews, letters and critical essays in which one accomplished practitioner criticises a specific passage of another accomplished practitioner, especially punctuation, diction, syntax, rhythm or register.

**Evidence packet per case:** critic, target, original text edition, precise disputed wording, direct criticism (short copyright-safe excerpt), what can be mechanically checked, whether critic's interpretation is contestable, later reception (separately sourced), and alternative readings.

**Avoid:** unsourced lists of famous insults; treating celebrity attacks as proof of bad writing; broad plot criticism substituting for actual prose; retroactive claims of technique not present in the critic's words.

**First scouting routes:** Nabokov's *Lectures on Literature* versus his fiction; Virginia Woolf's essays and diaries versus contemporaries; Mark Twain's criticism of James Fenimore Cooper; Henry James on other novelists; Ursula Le Guin's prose criticism. **These are leads only**, not yet verified micro-level conflicts. Retrieve exact source passages before creating conflict dossiers.
