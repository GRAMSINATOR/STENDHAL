# Brief R1 — news story

**Venue and reader:** the website of a local news organisation in the fictional city of Calder. General local readers; many will read only the first two paragraphs.

**Task:** write a news story of **300–400 words** reporting the vote below. Include a headline.

**Fixed facts (all fictional; use only these, and do not add others):**

- On Tuesday evening Calder City Council voted 7–4 to close two of the city's five branch libraries, Eastgate and Mill Lane.
- The closures take effect on 1 March.
- The council says the closures will save £1.2m a year. The total library budget is £9.8m a year.
- Last year Eastgate recorded 61,000 visits and Mill Lane 48,000. Central Library, the busiest remaining branch, recorded 310,000.
- Council leader Priya Nandakumar said: "We would rather run three libraries properly than five badly."
- Opposition councillor Joe Fairley, who represents the ward containing Eastgate, said: "Eastgate is the only place on that estate with free internet and a warm room."
- The council says a mobile library will visit each affected estate once a week. It has not published a timetable.
- The council has not said what will happen to the two buildings.

**Constraints:** do not invent further quotes, figures, people, reactions or background. Quotes must appear word for word. Write "the council says" (or an equivalent attribution) for claims the council makes.
