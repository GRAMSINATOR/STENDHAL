# W10 — Raymond Queneau: Exercises in Style (French 1947; English translation)

- **Territory:** 01 — Writers in the workshop
- **Source form:** primary experimental literary corpus
- **Research depth:** publisher description inspected; complete variants not analysed
- **Reference:** https://www.ndbooks.com/book/exercises-in-style/
- **Epistemic discipline:** The source-description claim is bounded by what was accessible. Tactical implications are STENDHAL hypotheses, **not attributed to the author as direct advice**.

## What the source establishes or invites us to study

Rewrites one mundane scenario in 99 substantially different linguistic/formal guises.

## Tentative tactical extraction — to test, not canonise

Build controlled transformations that operate on register, medium, poetic forms, syntax, time and speaker position independently or jointly.

## Polarities, tensions and countercases

Variation is not automatically artful meaning; compare style as experiment with deeply situated authorial composition.

## Next acquisition action

Choose four non-adjacent variants; compare conceptual shifts, not merely their visible differences.
