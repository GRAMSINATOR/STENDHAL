# Operator feedback → scoped learning and refactoring

The user/operator can challenge prose, a technique, a theoretical model, routing, profile fidelity, style-preservation rules or the architecture itself. Feedback is not automatically an instruction to rewrite the whole canon.

## Feedback event record (minimal, provisional)

- **Observation:** precise example or failure, with version/context.
- **Claim:** what the feedback asserts or hypothesises.
- **Altitude and reach:** lexical, sentence, paragraph, section, work; immediate or delayed consequences.
- **Context:** genre, audience, medium, selected worlds/styles, task.
- **Affected capability:** what could not be perceived, enacted, preserved or judged?
- **Scope:** local correction, regime/profile-specific, library-level, or architectural hypothesis.
- **Countercases:** where the correction may *not* apply.
- **Proposed change:** explicit target and dependencies.
- **Verification:** before/after cases or controlled test.
- **Disposition:** candidate / accepted locally / accepted broadly / rejected / superseded.
- **Rollback:** reference to prior decision and rationale.

## Workflow

Record → locate the smallest relevant owner → identify competing explanations → test controlled variants → revise **scopingly** → document why → probe adjacent regimes for regressions.

When feedback exposes a missing distinction, consider **splitting a model** or adding a new branch rather than adding another exception to a rule.

When feedback challenges the theory of composition itself, escalate to architecture review.

## Regression corpus

Maintain a small, evolving set of fixed examples and adversarial cases. The first historical case to import as **a case, not a norm**: AI-style cancellation suppressed legitimate explicit discourse markers, yielding airless passages. The remedy is a function-sensitive analysis of connectives, not a blanket permission or ban.

## Special cautions

- Absence of an operator objection is not evidence of quality.
- One observed failure does not establish universal causality.
- Conflicting preferences may be coherent within different writing philosophies.
- Improvements must be assessed against both **new gains** and **destroyed virtues**.
- Explicitly preserve operator disagreements and withdrawn hypotheses; don't bury them in accumulated prompts.
