# Virginia Woolf reads James Joyce's Ulysses

**Primary diary extracts and context:** Yale Modernism Lab https://campuspress.yale.edu/modernismlab/woolfs-reading-of-joyces-ulysses-1922-1941/ ; Morgan Library https://www.themorgan.org/exhibitions/online/ulysses/virginia-woolf-responds

**What she says:** In dated diary entries in 1922, Woolf first reports being interested and charmed by early parts, then becomes irritated and finally calls the book diffuse and overly showy. She grants it some genius and acknowledges her single, insufficiently attentive reading may have been unfair.

**Source status:** excerpted primary diary in scholarly or archival presentation, not the full notebooks. Some remarks depend on class assumptions about Joyce's upbringing; do not treat those as valid craft diagnostics.
