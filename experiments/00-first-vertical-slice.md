# Experiment zero — enact rhetorical turns without globalising the result

**State:** Proposed; no measured performance claims.

## Hypothesis

A structured but flexible capability model can improve deliberate execution of **paragraph-level turns** under dissimilar writing regimes, while preserving content and avoiding synthetic rhetorical templates.

## Test inputs

Create original, copyright-safe source materials with invariant facts:
- a practical technical explanation;
- an argumentative essay passage;
- a contemplative literary passage;
- a deliberately idiosyncratic or anti-conventional writing regime.

For each, include its preceding paragraph and the subsequent intended development. Avoid judging a turn without context.

## Protocol

1. Establish reader state, purpose and non-negotiable content.
2. Write neutral baseline.
3. Produce at least three turn variants with distinctly described intended effects, not merely sentence-length variants.
4. Independently inspect semantic advancement, rhetorical movement, rhythm, inference, attention, style fidelity and cross-paragraph dependence.
5. Vary one chosen feature while preserving a declared set of invariants.
6. Allow reflective critique to reject the use of a turn, the paragraph's role or the global plan.
7. Document the actual changes, lost qualities, uncertainty and competing plausible aesthetic evaluations.

## Observations to record

- Task fidelity: were facts and intended argument preserved?
- Distinct enactment: did variants genuinely perform different moves?
- Isolated control: could one parameter be varied without broad unintended rewriting?
- Cross-scale accounting: was the effect on nearby passages recognised?
- Plurality: did regimes yield truly different successful methods?
- Adversarial utility: did editorial critique produce a meaningful alternative rather than compulsive objection?
- Extraction usefulness: which hypotheses survived, failed or needed splitting?

Use quantitative proxies only where appropriate. Prefer **comparative observations with examples** to universal numerical craft scores.

## Exit condition

The experiment provides enough evidence to revise the enactive representation and propose the smallest viable **library/module interface**, including at least one principled disagreement about what successful execution means.
