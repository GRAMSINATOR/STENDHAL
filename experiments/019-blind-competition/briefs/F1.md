# Brief F1 — literary short fiction scene

**Venue and reader:** a literary magazine that publishes short fiction. Attentive adult readers.

**Task:** write a self-contained scene of **350–450 words**.

**Fixed situation:**

- Ruth, in her sixties, and her neighbour Tomasz, in his forties, live in adjoining houses.
- Yesterday evening they argued over the hedge on the boundary between their gardens.
- Some weeks ago Ruth borrowed Tomasz's aluminium extension ladder. This morning she carries it back. He is in his front garden.
- Close third person, from Ruth's point of view.
- The scene must end before either of them mentions the hedge or the argument.

**Open:** everything else, including what is said, what is noticed, tone and the ending.
