## Questions worth answering, without forcing a form

Which treatment actually deserves to exist? Which passage has real intellectual or literary movement? Which passage exhibits merely decorative craft? If one approach feels generic, is the problem vocabulary, rhetorical structure, source limitations, or an invalid commission?

## Important

Do not automatically update the library or style profiles because the operator dislikes one phrase. First determine whether the observation indicates a local failure, a condition-bound craft rule, or an architectural misconception. Never overwrite the original outputs; keep revision history.
