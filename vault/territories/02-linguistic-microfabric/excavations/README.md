The cards distinguish primary author/critic claims, attested examples, tentative observations, and missing corroboration. These sources address different **purposes and kinds of prose**; not every difference is a contradiction.

### Coverage now

Sound and lettering / syntax and balance / punctuation and continuity / clause placement and reader attention / micro-to-paragraph relations. Major missing territories: participial syntax, historical register, bilingual and translated prose, detailed diction in uncanonised genres, comma and dash manuscript variants, experimental oral performance, disciplined rhetorical misdirection in journalism, and actual systematic negative examples.

**No attempt is made to determine an exhaustive number of techniques in a sentence.**
