# Reviewers respond to Moby-Dick (1851)

**Sources:** 1851 review texts reprinted by Book Marks https://bookmarks.reviews/the-original-1851-reviews-of-moby-dick/ ; historical scholarship on recovered reviews https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-american-studies/article/abs/additional-unrecorded-reviews-of-melvilles-books/136F0140CD26EA67328D12AFD98A31A6

**What they say:** Henry F. Chorley's *Athenaeum* review condemns mixed genre, peculiar English, insufficiently controlled narrative construction, and ending. Other contemporary reviews praise the work's poetic and philosophical material while discussing its strangeness or objections. Reception was mixed, **not uniformly hostile**.

**Source status:** nineteenth-century reviews via modern transcriptions; original newspaper images not individually verified. Editions differ: British *The Whale* and American *Moby-Dick* cannot be assumed identical.
