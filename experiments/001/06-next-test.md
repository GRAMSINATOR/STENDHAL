# Next experiment — controlled transfer and genuinely divergent worlds

**Status:** Not run.

## Why

Experiment 001 was an unblinded self-pilot. It exposed likely convergence but did not measure gains attributable to a specific knowledge module or writing procedure.

## Recommended test

**Task:** same task materials, randomly alternated model conditions: (a) ordinary writing instructions, (b) STENDHAL capability context. Keep model, decoding constraints, context size as similar as practical.

**Source:** use an independently prepared, unseen factual scenario that is **not** another two-metric public-institution contradiction. Example: an engineering postmortem with a concrete timeline, conflicting witness notes, and an unresolved mechanism; do not prepare its final prose in the pilot authoring context.

**Worlds:** operational incident report; associative creative nonfiction; analytical essay that refuses standard suspense/revelation.

**Capture:** full prompts, versions, outputs and settings where available. Hide condition labels during human reading and do not treat this same model's ranking as independent evidence.

**Assessment:** separate factual fidelity, macrostructure, enactive control, regime differentiation, unwarranted inference, and tradeoffs. Prefer anchored pairwise judgments with reasons to an aggregate quality score.

**Adversarial probe:** at least one world must refuse the obvious information-order strategy, and one editorial response must be allowed to recommend *no article*.

**Promotion:** only candidate mechanisms that survive critical examination should move to the shared technique library. No claim of generality without new contexts.
