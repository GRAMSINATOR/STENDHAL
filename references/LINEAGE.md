# Source lineage

STENDHAL is a synthesis. It deliberately keeps one compact canonical brain rather than concatenating upstream style guides.

## Upstream repositories consulted

### stellarshenson/claude-code-plugins — popular-science skill
https://github.com/stellarshenson/claude-code-plugins/tree/main/plugins/datascience/skills/popular-science

MIT licensed.

Influence:
- structure before drafting;
- hook / nut-graf / abstraction-ladder thinking;
- named structure library;
- evidence paired with provenance and meaningful numbers;
- separate writer and adversarial reviewer.

STENDHAL generalizes these beyond popular science and does not copy the skill wholesale.

### opencue/cuecards — ai-slop-detector
https://github.com/opencue/cuecards/tree/main/.agents/skills/ai-slop-detector

MIT licensed.

Influence:
- distinguish synthetic texture from comprehension;
- density of patterns matters more than isolated vocabulary;
- diagnose buried ledes, missing throughlines, reader-assumption failures and telegraphic compression separately;
- adversarial editing after composition.

STENDHAL intentionally avoids pretending that an anti-slop score can identify authorship.

### joelio/plain-english — natural-writing
https://github.com/joelio/plain-english/tree/main/skills/natural-writing

MIT licensed.

Influence:
- reject formulaic enumeration and meta-commentary;
- avoid equal weighting of unequal points;
- prefer specificity to empty praise;
- use sentence-rhythm variation deliberately.

STENDHAL softens hard word bans into contextual warning signals.

### claytonroche/economist-writing-guide
https://github.com/claytonroche/economist-writing-guide

No license was found in the repository when consulted, so STENDHAL does not copy its text.

Influence at the level of general writing principles:
- concrete nouns and active verbs;
- old-to-new information flow;
- subject and verb early;
- source skepticism and primary-source preference;
- clear purpose/audience/message before drafting.

### ably/docs — writing-style-guide
https://github.com/ably/docs/blob/main/writing-style-guide.md

Apache-2.0 licensed.

Influence:
- technical accuracy outranks stylistic preference;
- active voice as a default rather than a religion;
- avoid subjective claims that can be replaced by demonstrable behaviour;
- lists and formatting should reflect actual information structure.

## User-supplied research memo

**Building a Real Editorial Voice for Mighty Andy** (September 2026).

This memo strongly influenced STENDHAL's distinction between publication "temperature" and publication machinery. Key generalized ideas:
- publications use different prose engines rather than one prestige voice;
- information order and scale changes are part of style;
- unusual language works through scarcity;
- short sentences are percussion, not a house style;
- paragraph endings need not continually summarize;
- editorial propositions should precede drafting;
- useful anti-slop editing audits abstraction, rhythm, repeated rhetorical templates, quirks, evidence and paragraph endings.

The memo's Mighty Andy-specific house rules were not treated as universal publication facts.
