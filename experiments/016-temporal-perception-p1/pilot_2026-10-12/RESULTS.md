### Actual passages: selected material

- S/B0's abstract departure: `the pool seemed to be waiting for an answer he had not been asked to give`. S/B2 instead focuses on damp plastic curling into the shape of an absent wrist, returning to mundane ventilation; not necessarily better.
- J/B0 states a sound distinction: `A city can make a bus more available and less dependable at the same time.` However, its multi-paragraph caveats also repeatedly explain why aggregates don't identify beneficiaries. J/B2 begins with the reporter watching a passenger steady a cake box, then moves into the same numbers and to a bounded route/time-of-night question.
- N/B0: `Her heartbeat grew loud enough to seem separate from her body.` N/B2: `Her hands were racing cold across the keys. The piano beating faster and faster beneath them.` The next dramatic intention concerns Clara's relation to a two-bar rest, not just restating she is anxious.

## Explicit B2 perception and intentions

Each B2 return records **exact source spans from B0**, observed properties, qualified alternate readings, the proposed new direction and options that would have taken a different route. For example:

- S notices that an extravagant waiting-pool image competes with an already available small physical oddity; **reframed** to pursue quiet material absence. Rival possibility: the odd larger metaphor may be more powerful.
- J notices a late passenger's cake box is observed but the rider's imagined background is **not evidence**; **reframed** to use the scene to make the data's epistemic limit concrete, not to invent an interview or passenger biography. Rival possibility: a direct data explainer may serve readers better.
- N notices that bodily pulse is explicitly named, the cold keys provide literal context, the assessor's cough has no demonstrated evaluative intention, and a notated rest can shift the protagonist's emotional objective; **reframed** to transfer pulse into piano and build pressure around silence. Rival possibility: the B0 direct account has more restraint.

**All observations and selected intentions were authored by the same assistant in one conversational context**. They are not independent evidence of genuine model cognition or reader experience.

## Local audit: 16/16 tests passed

Portable Python standard-library checks were **actually executed** for:
- nine distinct texts;
- precise perceptual source-witness strings present in B0;
- verbatim supplied fictional bus-driver quote;
- synthetic bus trial core figures preserved (24%, 81%, 72%, three routes, six-week);
- no obvious known false alternative statistics;
- no automatic physical-world piano flight in the psychologically expressive version;
- short-paragraph word bounds;
- explicit intention revision event types;
- JSON diff records and serialisability;
- no invented external readers or quality scores.

**A useful caught regression:** The first J/B2 draft inadvertently converted the supplied quote's original final full stop into a comma to attach its attribution. The exact-quote test failed, and B2 was corrected. This is a case where a normal “flow improvement” threatened a source contract. The checks **cannot** adjudicate semantic truth of all prose or aesthetic quality.

## Critical / non-winning observations

- **S:** B2's concrete wristband detail might be subtler than B0's abstract animation, but its `There was no mystery about that` may over-manage the reader; a good critic should consider retaining B0.
- **J:** B1 is shorter and arguably more suitable for a fast informational commission; B2's framing of its own evidential restraint can also sound self-conscious. B2 may move the cake image from observation to a later epistemic question; the reader response is unknown.
- **N:** B2's transferred predicates might create somatic expressionism but one long simile and final explicit psychological line may flatten an otherwise productive ambiguity. B0's understated ending could be preferable.

**The result is concrete differential writing plus a witness-grounded process trace—not proof that B2 wins.** In fact the pilot shows a live P1 may need an explicit **stop, leave alone, or branch back to B0** option.
