# Edmund Wilson reviews Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings

**Primary review:** Edmund Wilson, *Oo, Those Awful Orcs!*, *The Nation*, 14 April 1956; reproduction at https://www.jrrvf.com/sda/critiques/The_Nation.html

**What he says:** Wilson attacks diction, characterisation, the development of adventures, and his experience of the villains as insufficiently tangible or threatening. He discusses Sauron's distant presence, the Ring's premise, Frodo and Sam, and evaluates the trilogy's finale as flat. He also quotes praise from accomplished contemporaries, including W. H. Auden and C. S. Lewis, and distinguishes Tolkien's stated intentions from the inflated claims Wilson attributes to advocates.

**Source status:** full original review in a specialist reproduction, not the first magazine printing. The review contains contemptuous remarks about readers; these are part of the critic's stance, not factual statements about Tolkien's readers.
