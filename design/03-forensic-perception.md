# Forensic perceptual capacity — deliberately expensive before it becomes efficient

**Design state:** candidate architecture, prompted by operator critique on 2026-10-12. This is a *capacity research specification*, not a settled ontology or a validated detector.

## Prime directive

**Do not allow the library of named techniques to set the upper bound on what the system can perceive.**

A sentence may participate in fifteen, a hundred, or some incommensurable number of distinct expressive operations. Those counts depend on the analyst's granularity and model: they are neither authorial performance targets nor objective quantities we know how to establish.

The mission is unusually **high recall of defensible, significant distinctions**, followed by careful adjudication. Unlike an ordinary summary, an expert close-reading mode should be allowed to be painstaking, laborious, and expensive in time and context. Compression belongs downstream of attained understanding, not at the beginning.

## A crucial epistemic distinction

Separate observations at least provisionally into:
- **Surface trace:** a describable feature (word order, collocation, comma, phoneme pattern, recurrence).
- **Relational observation:** the feature's connection to other elements in nearby or distant contexts.
- **Functional hypothesis:** an interpretation of what the feature does.
- **Agency hypothesis:** a carefully qualified account of what may have been deliberately selected, improvised, habitually produced, collaboratively or editorially introduced, or merely encountered by a reader. These can overlap; neither perceived effectiveness nor the absence of recorded intention uniquely determines genesis.
- **Reader-effect hypothesis:** a proposed effect on attention, inference, emotion or perception, not yet independently observed.
- **Enactive affordance:** a property the generator might construct, influence, perturb, notice, preserve or allow to emerge without complete advance specification.
- **Technique candidate:** a possibly reusable and nameable operation; not every observation must be promoted to one.

One observed string may support multiple relations and readings; one proposed effect may depend on many surface traces; one alleged device may split into several at finer resolution. A missing name is not absence of mechanism.

## Operating idea: recursive close reading with discovery passes

**0. Acquire context.** Record genre, medium, surrounding passages, whole-work context where available, relevant language/culture, and explicit constraints. Track missing contexts. Never manufacture an author's intention.

**1. Unclassified noticing.** Before applying a technique inventory, enumerate conspicuous and inconspicuous textual decisions, tensions, asymmetries, absences and possible relationships. Permit observations for which no category exists. Retain raw notes.

**2. Independent microscopic passes.** Inspect: phonetic texture and stress; morphology; semantics; syntax and information structure; punctuation and pauses; collocation and register; viewpoint; deixis; negation and presupposition; timing and inference. These are *question prompts*, not a final taxonomy, and may expand.

**3. Orthogonal functional passes.** Re-examine the same spans via lexical-field crossings, rhetoric, argument, subtext, figurative networks, focalisation, reader models, attention, affect, irony, intertext, epistemic signalling, and intentional unconventionality.

**4. Nonlocal re-entry.** Expand outward: carryover from previous sentences, motif and echo across sections, delayed reinterpretations, distant lexical qualification, pattern breaks, structural constraints, interactions of recurring images or concepts. Then return to the word and inspect what context changed.

**5. Reverse-trace pass.** Start with a passage-wide or whole-work effect, then ask which nonexclusive local and global features might make that reading possible. A hypothesis is allowed to remain unresolved.

**6. Counterfactual passes.** Change or remove an adjective, connective, pause, structural position or recurrence; compare what survives and what breaks. Counterfactual textual changes test explanatory utility, but are not causal measurements of actual readers unless readers are studied.

**7. Adversarial pass.** Seek rival interpretations, invented patterns, unfounded claims of authorial intention, duplicate descriptions under different names, failure to notice ordinary-but-crucial structure, missing global context, and seductive but causally idle symbolism. Separate observation from imaginative projection.

**8. Coverage search.** Deliberately investigate what the existing analyses did *not* look for: alternate scales, different theoretical traditions, negative space, conditional affordances, historical associations, different reader repertoires. Ask what new question would produce a novel observation.

**9. Comparative consolidation.** Preserve competing readings and their warrants. Only after exploration may one build a compact working representation suitable for enaction. Don't collapse useful distinctions merely because an LLM context budget is scarce.

This procedure must be **branchable and recursive**, not a compulsory nine-step run for every writing task. The heavyweight setting is a research/evaluation mode; later production may load validated distilled capabilities.

## Coverage must be measured carefully

The true number of meaningful techniques in a work is not known and may not be well-defined. Consequently, no credible system can literally certify that it saw "all 100."

Approximations:
- **Expert discovery curves:** how many genuinely new, nonduplicative and defensible observations appear across further readings? Is the tail diminishing?
- **Cross-reader union:** gather independently generated annotations by readers trained in different traditions; examine what each perspective missed without declaring the union exhaustive.
- **Perturbation sensitivity:** when something changes in a text, does the apparatus notice expected collateral effects across scales? Does it hallucinate effects in negative controls?
- **Transfer usefulness:** does the extracted distinction enable controlled composition or correction in a new case, without breaking other values?
- **Precision and diversity:** count unsupported claims, collapsed mechanisms, and redundant labels as failures too.

A high count is not itself a success. A fashionable named device unsupported by the passage is a false positive. Reporting uncertainty and missing context is a perceptual strength.

## Open architectural questions

- What persistent data format best stores raw notices before categorisation without forcing a taxonomy?
- How can annotations from incompatible ontologies coexist without assuming they are synonyms?
- How do we record dependencies and interaction effects without the graph becoming impossibly large?
- Can a system recognise its analysis is prematurely saturated—because all passes use the same vocabulary or assumptions?
- Which controlled perturbations distinguish real mechanisms from retrospective interpretations?
- How can the deepest descriptive mode feed a *nonformulaic* enactive generator rather than turning into a thousand mechanical obligations?

## Relationship to STENDHAL's three capabilities

**Perception and extraction:** deliberately overcomplete discovery and cautious explanation.

**Enaction:** reserve freedom to compose, perturb and recast across overlapping tracks. A writer may operate on interdependent mechanisms without enumerating them at runtime.

**Reflective/editorial:** adversarial challenge to the *entire chosen approach*, to aesthetic criteria, and to mistaken causal explanations; may protect productive deviations from reductive edits.

Do not infer that skilled writers consciously performed the annotation. The apparatus models affordances and possible effects, not their private experience.


## Non-intentional effects are still real (operator correction, 2026-10-12)

The forensic apparatus must not convert its interest in agency into an intention-first filter. A critic can discover and defend a meaningful pattern without evidence it was consciously chosen. Conversely, source/witness uncertainty still matters for accurately identifying *which* material is being read; neither accidental origin nor absent authorial testimony negates an observed effect. Writing processes may recursively alter intention through outcomes, and the result may outrun the initial plan.

Evaluate creative effects in their own contexts rather than making authorial fidelity the default tribunal. Preserve precision about task-bound obligations (science, contractual claims, faithful copyediting) without imposing them as the aesthetic purpose of all writing. Fine enactive capacity includes recognition and retention of productive uncontrolled aspects, not only selective manipulation. See [parallel stream correction](../vault/parallel-streams/2026-10-12-02-intention-outcome-autopoiesis-and-partial-control.md).

## Perception as the active steering organ of a transformative process (2026-10-12)

**M03 integration:** [transformative composition](provisional-models/M03-transformative-composition-process.md) proposes a recurrent kernel: **generative intention → enaction → observed material → perceptual return → reaction/transform → revised or branched intention**. The first experimental traces are in [EXP-012](../experiments/012-multilayer-process-studio/README.md). This is a proposed harness behaviour, not a claim that human writers perform explicit sequential introspective steps.

An exquisite perceptual return must do more than provide stylistic adjectives. It must sometimes identify, with *actual textual warrants*:

1. **Witness:** what material changed, at which exact span and witness/version (including punctuation, word placement and pronoun/tense ownership)?
2. **Relations:** which sound, syntactic, semantic, perspectival, spatial or nonlocal relationships have appeared, moved, weakened or disappeared?
3. **Inferences:** what is *said*, what is only *inferable* in particular context, and what can remain undecided? Which new relation depends on a specific reader's knowledge?
4. **Collateral:** what did the change sacrifice? Did a literary gain also invent a physical act, change quantification, break a rule of scene geometry, or introduce a new ambiguous voice?
5. **Potential:** which next primitive transformations become available because of this change? Is a bad-looking surplus valuable as raw material, or is an irregularity worth protecting?
6. **Critical alternatives:** does an independent reading refute the effect? Could a simpler sentence, or no revision, be better? What does the analyst *not* know?
7. **Intention impact:** is the current aim supported, undermined, split, or made obsolete by what the text now does? **The action may be to change the aim itself.**

**These are questions that can be asked when relevant, not seven obligatory labels per sentence or a fixed quantitative score.** A model that confidently invents beautiful explanations of effects that are not supported by the page is less perceptive than one identifying a precise modest relation and its limits.

### Example: cold pianist

Source conditions: cold instrument, distressed pianist, rising physiological pulse and playing tempo.

- Witness change: `cold piano` → `hands raced cold across the keys`; `heart beating faster` → `piano beating faster`.
- A perceptive return should notice **distribution of coldness and beat**, and that the transferred version **no longer literally asserts** cold keys or fast heartbeat. It may evoke these facts by inference from a prior open door or other setup, but that is not automatic.
- It should inspect whether the fragmentary `The piano beating...` contributes to felt continuation or creates unwanted syntactic stumbling.
- It should distinguish the dramatic invention `She took neither` (at two bars of rest) from the prior source facts. This is admissible in fictional exploration but is **new story content**, not a stylistic re-expression of identical facts.
- It can suggest a new intention: *make the player's struggle with stopping the centre of the scene*, even though the original aim focused on stress/temperature.
- It can also **decline** to change intention and keep the direct description as a different appropriate work.

### Why this may improve actual LLM prose

A generator often has ample fluency and access to plausible continuations. The missing capability might not be ignorance of the abstraction itself but insufficient *active perceptual re-entry* into what its own expression actually entails, evokes and affords across scales. This is a **candidate explanatory hypothesis**, not an established result for all LLMs. The loop should test whether external independent inspection and later recomposition produce changes impossible for a simple additional drafting budget.

### How to reject the perceptual module

- It consistently notices symbolic ghosts but misses grammatical/physical facts.
- Its observations do not cause concrete, independently checkable changes in subsequent creative options.
- It always recommends simplification or elegance regardless of the regime.
- It mistakes detailed post-hoc explanation for actual source or audience evidence.
- It forces a fragile tacit writing process into an exhausting bureaucracy.
- It cannot identify that the most appropriate next action is **no action** or **abandon the objective**.

**Process requirement:** every *experimentally asserted* perceptual insight should link to a visible witness and a falsifiable downstream opportunity or decision. No requirement that writers or generators explicitly perform such a log in everyday drafting.
