# Critical disagreements about Dickens's Hard Times

**Primary critical essays in reproduction:**
- F. R. Leavis, *Hard Times: An Analytic Note* (1948): https://www.enotes.com/topics/hard-times/criticism/criticism/f-r-leavis-essay-date-1948
- David H. Hirsch (1964): https://www.enotes.com/topics/hard-times/criticism/criticism/david-h-hirsch-essay-date-1964
- John Holloway (1962): https://www.enotes.com/topics/hard-times/criticism/criticism/john-holloway-essay-date-1962
- David Lodge (1966): https://www.enotes.com/topics/hard-times/criticism/criticism/david-lodge-essay-date-1966

**What they say:** Leavis elevates *Hard Times* for its seriousness and integrated expressive command; Hirsch contests its artistic accomplishment; Holloway stresses historical contexts essential to judgment; Lodge examines its rhetoric and distinguishes formal success from the ranking of Dickens's novels. Their arguments cannot be reduced to a single high-level binary.

**Source status:** reproductions of sustained critical essays; the authoritative original printings were not separately checked.
