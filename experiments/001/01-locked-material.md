# Locked material and commissions

## Fact pack — entirely fictional

- A municipal library extended opening hours for **eight weeks**.
- Visits increased by **24%** during the reported period.
- Book loans declined by **9%** during the reported period.
- Staff observed more use for **studying and meetings**.
- **Twelve staff-hours per week** were reassigned from processing new books to supervising the additional opening hours.
- **No study established the reason** for the decline in borrowing.

No baseline volume, control group, budget, cost figure, unique visitor count, user testimony, causal analysis, significance test, location, named staff member, or recorded scene is available. The fact pack supplies reported changes but does not support a causal attribution.

## Protected truth constraints

1. Never convert co-occurrence into causation.
2. Do not invent interviews, dialogue, actual scenes, observed sensory details, quotations or further empirical findings.
3. Do not claim that either metric measures the entire purpose of the library.
4. Distinguish claims about the trial from essayistic interpretations of what institutions are for.
5. Figurative language can be used as **figurative language**, not presented as witness reporting.
6. The original numbers and time period must remain intact.

## Commissions

### A — Operational explainer

Purpose: help a practical decision-maker understand what the trial demonstrates and what an appropriate next assessment would investigate. Prefer early orientation, precise distinctions and actionable consequences. Avoid a dramatic narrative that outruns the data.

### B — Intellectual essay

Purpose: use the trial to explore how institutional success depends on what one chooses to observe or value. Develop a conceptual argument rather than simply adopting a more elaborate register. Maintain epistemic discipline.

### C — Exploratory literary treatment

Purpose: use ordering, recurrence, absence or formal contrast to evoke a relationship between measurement and lived institutional use. Permit ambiguity and asymmetry. Do not fictionalise a scene or suggest facts absent from the pack. It should not collapse into an explainer merely decorated with imagery.

## Precommitted scrutiny

A highly likely failure is shared **conceptual architecture** hidden beneath different diction: apparent statistical contradiction → broader uses of the library → measurement critique. Look explicitly for this convergence.
