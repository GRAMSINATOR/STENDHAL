# Style profile schema

Every publication profile in STENDHAL describes a recurring editorial engine, not a costume.

Use these fields:

- **Information engine**
- **Entry point**
- **Sequencing**
- **Compression**
- **Scene allowance**
- **Authorial presence**
- **Judgement**
- **Lexical markedness**
- **Humour / wit**
- **Sentence movement**
- **Evidence cadence**
- **Ending**
- **Best suited to**
- **Failure mode when imitated badly**

When applying a profile, preserve the factual ledger and the chosen article format. Change structure before diction.

Publication profiles are approximations derived from editorial guidance and close reading. They do not imply that every article from a publication follows one house formula.
