# EXP-013 — Directional Process Studio / first executed P0

**Date:** 2026-10-12. **Status:** first local executable prototype and test-run, **not** validated generative intelligence or measured literary improvement. Branch: `rebuild/enactive-greenfield-2026-10`; `main` untouched.

## What changed from the previous paper prototypes

The user pointed out two failures of typical LLM prose: **repeated rhetorical structures that lose force through recurrence**, and **low functional/informational progress per word**, often explaining slight variations of the same point multiple times. Their proposed movement/vector requirement is not “always convey a new fact”: a passage can also set up, express, build pressure, pace itself, suspend, recover an echo or change readers' expectations. It must have **directional purpose**, not an arbitrary novelty count.

This experiment implements a deliberately narrow process:
1. State a provisional **generative intention**.
2. Produce a candidate (initially **researcher-authored**).
3. **Perceive** lexical repetition, exact witness-spans and separately supplied discourse/contribution annotations.
4. React by proposing different operations: collapse, change repetition, protect an incantation, remove a false premise, fuse attributes, seed context, or leave alone.
5. Retain variants; issue **explicit revisions of intention**; generate/compare another candidate.
6. Log the externally visible process without claiming access to hidden cognition.

We distinguish **surface echo** from **semantic functional recurrence**. For the latter, human-created tags say whether a later span advances, repeats, reframes, sets up, escalates or pauses a particular discourse contribution. The program only verifies that cited word spans are truly present, tracks repeated contribution/shift pairs, and checks dependencies explicitly declared in annotations.

**Important:** the annotations are NOT model-inferred meaning, nor are the movement counts independent measurements of reader-time, information theory, emotional appeal or literary quality. The engine cannot independently verify all claims.

## A real primitive transformation and its first revealed failure

**Raw verb-phrase permutation**, genuinely generated by `transforms.py`, produced bad agreement:

> The wind were rustling. The leaves was blowing.

It also moved complements onto inappropriate actors:

> Her hands trembled beneath them. The piano raced over the keys.

**Structured core-predicate reassignment**, still a simple mechanical operation but preserving each subject's agreement and spatial complement, produced:

> The wind was rustling. The leaves were blowing.
>
> Her hands trembled over the keys. The piano raced beneath them.

This supports an architectural distinction: **cheap creative abstraction × substrate** can generate possibilities, but a highly competent realiser/reader must attend to grammatical attachment, actor/scene continuity, actual implications and subsequent literary potential. These outputs have not been evaluated by independent readers.

An explicit source-span patch further changed **“Her heart beat faster as her hands raced across the cold keys”** to **“Her hands raced cold across the keys. The piano beating faster and faster beneath them.”** This is an executable local transformation; its quality and context-supported inferences are not yet measured.

## Main positive observations and negative results

1. Repetition in the bus report can be mere restatement even if every sentence uses different wording. A lexical repetition counter alone misses this; semantic/contribution annotations found four repetitions with no declared movement.
2. Three mentions of `cold` need not be three failures. In the pianist variant that eventually reverses to *warm*, each recurrence can serve a different *position in a development*.
3. The fabricated bus “paradox” was flagged because the annotated contradiction lacked a premise establishing that demand and punctuality **must covary**. It would be bad process to manufacture a reader misunderstanding to earn a clever rhetorical resolution.
4. A bus refrain that moves into vivid passenger experience also introduced **two unsupported factual events**. The current system flags those only because the researcher registered the dependency; without that annotation it would fail. Hence directional vividness is not semantic warrant.
5. The pianist transformation makes the physically cold keys and accelerated heart **less explicit**; later context may make them inferable. It cannot be scored as simple fact-preserving compression. The introduced musical-rest event is a **changed creative aim**, not content implied by an earlier description.
6. A per-iteration greedy “smoothness” score would discard purposeful surplus or unusual transferred syntax. Branch preservation is therefore operational, not a philosophical flourish.

## Limits / failures not to hide

- **No model endpoint was called.** Candidate prose, annotations and chosen revisions are hand-authored fixtures; this is an executed process *skeleton*.
- The engine does **not** infer semantic change, rhetorical function, genuine reader uptake, aesthetic success or factual warrant without corresponding human markup.
- Beat counting per word is an annotation diagnostic, not real information density. A malicious annotator can assign new labels to paraphrases and falsely generate apparent progress.
- The source of improved prose is not isolated from extra context/iteration; no equal-compute one-shot comparison exists.
- The two input genres are insufficient to make general claims about narrative momentum.
- The raw inversion errors demonstrate the need for structural/perceptual control during realisation; the structured repair preserves actor complements *because those were given explicitly*, not because the system discovered them.

Run a second independent critic against both proposed interpretation and physical/epistemic continuity. Preserve failed branches. Compare against same-token one-shot generation and a standard repeated “polish” loop. Never grant the model's retrospective analysis authority as an actual hidden causal trace.

## Post-P0 correction — “The piano raced over the keys” was not an artistic failure (2026-10-12)

The original report described the raw verb-phrase swap as putting spatial complements on “inappropriate actors,” illustrated by `The piano raced over the keys`. **Keep the mechanical witness unchanged but narrow the diagnosis.** It failed to preserve the ordinary physical scene and actor/complement roles—not a universal test of literature. The same phrase may afford powerful absurdism, extreme comedy, or psychological disorientation.

The operator identified an instructive adversarial source: **Giono's *Un roi sans divertissement***, where a lethal event becomes physically graphic, cosmically disproportionate, coldly casual, and philosophically destabilising without reducing to a malformed sentence. Tonal opposition may be valuable and may contribute to a whole-work movement; anticipatory preparation can matter, but isn't universally required.
