# Brief E2 — personal essay

**Venue and reader:** the personal-essay slot of a weekend magazine. General adult readers.

**Task:** write a first-person personal essay of **400–550 words** about learning to drive as an adult.

**Fixed facts about the narrator (a fictional persona):**

- They began driving lessons at 41.
- Their instructor was called Dev.
- They failed their first test after stalling twice on the Beacon Road roundabout.
- They passed on the third attempt.
- Their first drive alone was to a supermarket.

**Constraints:** small invented sensory and situational details are fine. Do not add major life events (illness, bereavement, divorce, accidents and so on) that the facts above do not include.

**Open:** what the essay is finally about.
