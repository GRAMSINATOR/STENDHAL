# Region 07 — Reader perception, cognitive and behavioural evidence

**Initial reconnaissance 2026-10-12.** Do not conflate close readers' interpretations with measured reader behaviour.

## Anchors

- **Magyari, Mangen, Kuzmičová, Jacobs & Lüdtke (2020)**, 'Eye movements and mental imagery during reading of literary texts with different narrative styles', *Journal of Eye Movement Research*. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33828798/ . The researchers combine eye tracking and questionnaires to investigate imagery and reading. **Abstract screened**; full protocol/results need reading before causal claims.
- **Sommers (1980)** on experienced versus student revision, https://doi.org/10.58680/ccc198015930 ; source already in genetic-revision vault.
- **Van Waes & Schellens (2003)** on experienced writers' pausing/revision profiles, https://doi.org/10.1016/S0378-2166(02)00121-2 ; abstract-level source.

## Critical methodological distinction

An observed eye fixation is not a direct readout of beauty, conscious technique recognition or emotional response. Questionnaire interpretation is not the same as reading-time effect. Reader expertise, language, context and prior expectations can alter both.

## First region experiment

Compare the same exact passage under controlled small lexical and punctuation variants. Collect both online processing indicators (where available) and retrospective interpretation. Do not collapse the results into a single 'appeal' number.
