# STENDHAL — Writing Research Vault

**Status:** Research acquisition, not canonical craft instructions.
**Opened:** 2026-10-12.
**Scope:** seven deliberately heterogeneous territories; Territory 01 is the first deep-entry batch. Others are seeded, not falsely described as deeply read.

The purpose is to **map the world of writing before reducing it into a compact, executable substrate**. We are studying articulate and tacit craft, named techniques and unnamed operations, conscious process and emergent form, empirical reader effects, failed writing and adversarial disagreements.

## Working constraints

1. **Never mistake a taxonomy for an ontology of writing.** The same text can have many intertwined functions over radically different spans.
2. Maintain source form: author's own account ≠ editor-selected correspondence ≠ manuscript scholar ≠ review ≠ psycholinguistic experiment.
3. Preserve polarity: *intra-territory disagreement* is a research target, not a nuisance to harmonise.
4. Record **bad writing** and bad criticism with equal seriousness; don't ban words when the problem is conceptual warrant.
5. Do not overstate coverage. "Indexed" is not "read"; "read" is not "understood"; "understood" is not "enactable"; "enactable" is not "good".
6. No wholesale copyrighted source copying. Keep accurate citation metadata, links, concise quotation where necessary, and original analytical notes.
7. Use bilingual/multilingual and culturally distinct sources as the vault expands; do not flatten translation choices into an author's original microcraft.
8. No normative taste ranking or one universal quality score.

**This vault does not replace the enactive workbench.** It discovers potential instruments, conflicts and questions; only subsequent experiments justify promoting any extraction into working capabilities.

These tracks remain separate until evidence establishes useful relationships. None prescribes a vector space or compulsory number of analytic passes.
