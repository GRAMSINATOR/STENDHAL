# EXP-017 / 01 — Two source-grounded craft profiles (not two universal styles)

**2026-10-13. Pilot source cards; provisional.** Source extraction is not a claim that a living writing intelligence has emerged or that all effects were consciously planned. Each profile is an **available local method and lens**, not a set of forced stylistic characteristics. This is a deliberate methodological contraction after the failed large-scene EXP-016.

## A — Austen, *Emma*, volume III chapter VII (chapter 43): social consequence and permeable narration

**Primary witness:** [*Emma*, chapter 43](https://www.janeausten.org/emma/chapter-43.php); independent [alternate transcription](https://www.online-literature.com/austen/emma/43/). Compare repository [close reading E03-02](../../vault/territories/03-narrative-subtext/excavations/E03-02-austen-emma-carriage-voice-transition.md). Online witnesses differ at one small `his`/`this` pronoun locus; **do not** infer authorial intent from the discrepancy without copy-text collation.

**Observed, not imagined:**
- Knightley's criticism and Emma's failed opportunity to acknowledge it are **specific social events**, not generic invented philosophical themes.
- The carriage moves away while Emma tries to look back: failed action and increasing distance give her shame a physical temporal substrate.
- `with what appeared unusual speed`: a judgment of travel speed can be read as filtered through Emma's current experience without literally asserting that the horses accelerated.
- `How could she have been so brutal`: character-centred exclamation enters third-person narratorial material without an explicit `she thought` delimiter.
- Narratorial reporting and character-inflected self-reproach **mix and change proximity across sentences**, then return to a calmer report of the journey.

**A possible small transferable capability:** Given a *specific materially supported* social conflict, permit narrated action to carry the developing viewpoint; let the apparent ownership of evaluation shift locally where the event warrants it. A short physical action can be a better carrier than a new analytical sentence declaring what the event symbolises.

**When NOT to invoke:** a technical explanation without a dramatised subject; an introspective monologue already doing the work; a scene lacking any material social consequence; a project where reported first-person thoughts must not be invented; or a passage that is better left with externally observed action.

**Likely counterfailure:** generate hollow free-indirect questions solely because the source uses rhetorical questions; borrow Austen's carriage/window/kindness plot rather than the observed relation; convert the method into an all-purpose psychology engine.

**What can be perceived elsewhere without copying Austen:** narrator/character interpretive proximity, actual event versus inward evaluation, delay between action and understanding, physical movements carrying emotionally consequential timing, or the **absence** of any need for internal focalisation.

## B — Giono, *Un roi sans divertissement*: habitual fact reclassified, then disproportionate consequence

**Primary ending excerpt:** [French educational reproduction](https://firminettherese.blogspot.com/2019/02/4e-sequence-giono-un-roi-sans_65.html); comparison and contextual study [Magister](https://www.site-magister.com/roi1.htm); alternate [scholarly account of narrative ellipsis](https://lodelpreprod.univ-rennes2.fr/motifs/index.php?id=948); prior repository [Research 10](../../research/10-giono-cosmic-rupture-tonal-consequence.md). **Only the ending excerpt and secondary context were checked—not a fresh full-novel reading.**

**Observed, not imagined:**
- A domestic evening custom and a small visible light are materially established.
- What appeared to be the burning tip of a cigar is **reclassified** as a dynamite fuse: the same sensory cue changes its meaning after a new fact is supplied.
- A brief physically legible lethal event receives a luminous description. The outrageous final scale and philosophical question do not arise merely from arbitrary metaphor; analysis of the wider novel finds earlier interactions of ritual, violence, comedy and beauty.
- The narrator phrases the reconstruction as something Langlois **must have done**; not every part is omniscient verified fact.
- An alternative scholarly interpretation sees the Pascal connection as satirical rather than as a straightforward philosophical answer. No single explanation monopolises the passage.

**A possible small transferable capability:** Let a *concrete* already-seen element become something different when the reader discovers a withheld physical fact. That is not a universal instruction to add a death metaphor or a cosmic flourish.

**When NOT to invoke:** the original detail has no second meaning; a routine object is simply routine; no material warrant allows a reversal; the surrounding work does not justify changing scale; a factual report may not invent a dramatic secret.

**Likely counterfailure:** borrow dynamite or blood imagery regardless of subject; add grand ironic wisdom; write an incongruous cosmic finale to a trivial event; mistake shock for earned movement.

**What can be perceived elsewhere without performing the trick:** hidden dependency between an observation and later evidence, how a late fact changes an earlier object, source uncertainty, abrupt vs prepared tonal rupture, and the possibility that no reclassification exists.

## These profiles are intentionally not blended

Austen's focal-proximity passage and Giono's radically reclassified object do **not** combine into a synthetic master method. A shared perceiver can inspect either text for material grounding, expectation and shifts of apparent meaning; that does not imply a writer must combine their preferred tools in one paragraph.

**Experimental constraint:** Source study can enlarge **perception** broadly. Active use must be selected, declined or deferred on the evidence of the *particular target*, not because its profile is loaded. Only a 35–70-word micro-span will be written per target initially. No mandatory phonological, thematic, tonal or lexical interventions.

## Interruption / independent cross-check (2026-10-13)

The operator corrected the assumption that a source-derived skill corresponds to one isolable visible device. In strong passages, **many perceptual observations can converge** on the same tiny decision, which might itself emerge from a coherent vision or process rather than separable interventions. A source profile should thus record **overlapping and uncertain explanations with one shared text witness**, *not* emit every detected technique. The needed action may be a singular choice or no edit.

See the subsequent [cross-source challenge](03-convergent-perception-and-restraint.md), including Proust's vision, Hemingway's qualified omission, Poe's purposeful effect, Woolf's shifting experiential form, Austen's fused voice, and genuinely opposed cases of conspicuous foregrounding and deliberate repetition. Nothing in this correction makes unobtrusive writing universally preferable.
