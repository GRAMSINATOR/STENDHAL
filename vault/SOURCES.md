### Acquisition provenance caveat

Most entries are initial publisher records, scholarly abstracts, university-hosted primary pages or author/publisher descriptions verified on 2026-10-12. A few accessible primary passages were directly examined, but no purchased books were wholly read during this acquisition pass.

### Unique-source handling

An individual work may appear in several territories because territory membership is **a navigation view**, not a claim of exclusive ontology. The detailed Territory 01 card is its provisional source-of-truth when that entry occurs in Territory 01; later unique IDs can support machine retrieval and graph links if justified.

### Priority pending sources / access

- Bukowski letters by date and exact edition, not second-hand slogans.
- Dostoevsky notebook images and his own letters in reliable translations/original.
- Orwell's own caveats; refutations of his linguistic prescriptions.
- A genuine range of non-Western, non-English, oral and popular craft traditions.
- Empirical examples of both effective difficulty and actual incoherence.
- Verified original text and context for future line-level quotation; avoid bulk copying.
