# M04 — Scoped tonal field: layered logics and radical expressive choices

**STENDHAL / 2026-10-12. TEMPORARY MODEL.** Not an aesthetic law, vector-space commitment, genre classifier or literary result. An operator-originated candidate for what [M03](M03-transformative-composition-process.md) could manipulate and what [M01](M01-expressive-ecology.md) could perceive.

## Starting observation

**Radical decisions can generate extraordinary stylistic distinctness.** Constraining a layer of writing changes which choices are available and which deviations become consequential. Genre, voice and tone often impose such constraints, but a work is neither obliged to occupy one tonal category nor to keep every constraint in force everywhere.

The operator's concrete example: a spacefaring science-fiction story whose physical/engineering causality is rigorously realistic, while characters' emotional experience is rendered with impossible, expressionist metaphors, and a single character speaks with deadpan absurdism.

**Consequence:** “realism versus absurdity” is not one global dial. These properties can coexist in different aspects of a single scene.

## Different logics may govern different aspects

- **World ontology:** what actually exists and happens.
- **Causality/physics:** what physical events are possible and what explanations are warranted.
- **Narration and epistemic status:** whether a claim is asserted as fact, metaphor, focalised experience, dialogue, mistaken perception, reported uncertainty, or left unresolved.
- **Voice and social stance:** who's speaking or colouring the passage; the narrator and different characters can make incompatible claims or use different idioms.
- **Emotional/expressionist material:** which bodily properties can migrate into objects, rhythm and atmosphere.
- **Temporal and rhetorical structure:** pacing, buildup, interruption, recurrence and release; even repeated constructions may develop rather than stall.
- **Reader expectations and information:** which conventions are established and what the reader can recover or reconsider.
- **Genre and local contract:** differing permissions and hard requirements; a given scene can reject the dominant book-level convention.

This list is **a set of discrimination prompts, not a required schema**. Physical impossibility, narrator reliability, and expressive intensity are *different kinds* of property; some may be categorical, relational, uncertain or plural. Numerical coordinates may become useful locally but should not erase their differences.

## Typed scope, not a magical global tone score

A provisional layered record:

```yaml
world.physics: strict
world.engineering: physically_caused
narrator.register: cold_precise
emotion.expression: metaphorical
character.Jax.voice: absurd_deadpan
scene.exception: possible_sudden_tonal_rupture
utterance:
  material: "The radiator applauded."
  claimed_status: character_speech # or metaphor, perception, literal, unresolved
```

The same utterance can be a true impossible event in an absurd physical world, an impossible **assertion** in a strict world, a figurative sentence compatible with strict physics, a distressed character's perception, a joke, or a sustained ambiguity. No one literal string fixes those alternatives without context.

### Real collisions and real choices

- **Hard constraint collision** is a statement relative to a given commission/world claim: a literal instrument running across its own keys is inconsistent with an explicitly ordinary physical event. The collision can remain in the draft as a possible intentional break; do not promote that to a global bad-writing verdict.
- **Soft stylistic collision** may be an artistically productive rupture, a failed joke or merely unfamiliar language. Reading context and reader response matter.
- **Cross-scale mismatch** can serve a large-scale payoff even when it disrupts the local surface. Giono's *Un roi sans divertissement* and its cold absurd-cosmic final expansion is a source-level adversary to a universal “tonal cohesion” check: see [Research 10](../../research/10-giono-cosmic-rupture-tonal-consequence.md).
- **No guaranteed foreshadowing:** the system may prepare an exception over time, or introduce it with startling abruptness. “Unprepared” cannot imply “invalid.”

## M04 is NOT the harness

The operator sharpened this while M04 was being written: the foundational goal is **multiple competing approaches assembled into nested, reusable decision structures**. Ask whether to impose a radical constraint first; whether stylistic colouring is wanted; what layer it belongs to; which operations and evaluative lenses fit; whether the work should keep or overturn its own commitments.

The ordering must contain **real dependency edges**, not one compulsory checklist: an enforcement action requires a declared target/claim status, while deciding on voice before physics can be perfectly legitimate. Questions and operators themselves must be movable/revisable. See [M05 — adaptive decision topology](M05-adaptive-craft-decision-topology.md) and the executable [EXP-015](../../experiments/015-reconfigurable-decision-harness/README.md).

## How to break M04

- A system that decides “absurd” for a character and then allows every literal physical impossibility in the world has confused scopes.
- A hard-science story can still use highly irrational emotional images; rejecting them would be misapplied constraint.
- A magical world can have emotionally plain narration, so permissive physics does not imply florid style.
- One coldly narrated fantastic event can be more coherent than continuous expressive shouting.
- A well-established tonal rule may be broken once, without explicit foreshadowing, for powerful effect. Do not make local dissonance an error score.
- A model that assigns arbitrary numbers to heterogeneous properties then explains all choices retrospectively has not established that its vector space helps generation or criticism.
