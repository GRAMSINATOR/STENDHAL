# EXP-016 pilot — witnessed perceptions and newly issued intentions

**Same assistant context, not blind.** All source strings below occur in the preserved B0 examples, and each perceiving claim has an alternative interpretation. This is the decision trace that guided the three actual [B2 passages](B2-actual-revisions.md), not evidence of reader reception or internal model cognition. For the complete 13 individual source-witness observations, alternatives, source passages and deterministic checks see the accompanying downloadable ZIP.

## S: a restrained empty pool

**B0 material:** `No one was inside now.` doubles the already known fact that the swimmer departed; it may be flat restatement, or a consciously uneasy reconfirmation. `The wristband had not moved` is an actual stable physical detail that can carry quiet attention. `the pool seemed to be waiting for an answer he had not been asked to give` introduces large abstract animation without much preparation; but an unprepared uncanny departure could be exactly right.

**Changed generative intention:** stop asking for overt uncanniness; see if a concrete physical detail and the decision of what to do with it can carry the feeling while the register stays restrained.

**Actual B2 choice:** damp band curls like the absent wrist; the ordinary ventilation closes. **Live counterreading:** the B0's stranger waiting-pool phrase might be much more powerful. B2's explicit `There was no mystery` could deflate what B2 managed to create.

## J: fake-data reported chronicle

**B0 material:** `an appreciation is not a result` is justified but similarly phrased caution occurs several times. `A city can make a bus more available and less dependable at the same time` genuinely distinguishes two measured dimensions instead of asserting a contradiction. `The passenger held the box flat on their knees` is a concrete witnessed behaviour, **not** evidence of where they were going or why. The exact fictional quote `People don't stop working when the buses do.` argues for studying late-service demand but isn't proof of this pilot's benefits.

**Changed generative intention:** let the one witnessed scene mark the difference between measured boarding and useful, understood journey. Introduce figures as a hinge, not a plot twist; end with a narrower research question. No invented passenger dialogue/biography.

**Actual B2 choice:** eyewitness opening → data distinction → driver's observed wait and supplied quotation → route/time-of-night question → return to the box and the reporting limit. **Live counterreading:** B1's shorter, conventional article may better serve an informational reader. B2 still repeats several epistemic cautions and risks writing a rhetorical essay about journalistic uncertainty.

## N: expressionist but physically possible audition

**B0 material:** `The keys felt cold as soon as she touched them` supplies literal tactile grounding. `Her heartbeat grew loud enough to seem separate from her body` reports embodied anxiety directly but offers a transfer candidate. `One of the assessors coughed` is real in the fictional source; Clara's inference of judgment remains an **interpretation**. `At the foot of the first page came two bars of rest` already makes a transition from playing to required silence available. `She had stopped at the right place` preserves an event where she does *not* miss the rest.

**Changed generative intention:** rather than restate faster tempo/pulse, explore a pianist whose instrument carries bodily acceleration and who becomes frightened of the silence itself. Preserve the score's actual pause, ordinary physical laws and the judges' unknown intentions.

**Actual B2 choice:** `Her hands were racing cold across the keys. The piano beating faster and faster beneath them`, followed by the approaching notated rest and a correct entry afterward. **Live counterreading:** a later extended simile and overt statement of emotional insecurity may blunt the powerful transferred physical experience. The B0 scene may need no stylistic intensification.

## Stopping condition

The returns do not declare that any B2 is better. They identify **plausible new intentions** along with reasons not to pursue them. A future truly independent P1 must test whether a perceiver finds these relations without knowing which rewrite the generative experimenter expects, and whether human readers experience the proposed movement.
