# EX03 — Nabokov: prior pattern, nonsequential assembly, and the diagnosis of counterfeit profundity

**Primary:** Vladimir Nabokov, *The Art of Fiction No. 40*, interviewed by Herbert Gold, *The Paris Review*, Summer–Fall 1967. https://www.theparisreview.org/interviews/4310/the-art-of-fiction-no-40-vladimir-nabokov
**Depth:** direct first-pass primary close reading of interview sections, C2. Interviews were supplied to Nabokov in advance, his answers were written, and he reworked questions; this is a crafted self-presentation, not transparent introspection.

## Relevant primary anchors

- Asked whether he follows a planned sequence, he describes knowing a broad pattern, then filling individual sections in variable order using index cards.
- In a nearby discussion of **poshlost**, he attacks imitation, cliché and bogus intellectual profundity as part of a broad value-laden concept with Russian cultural specificity.
- He mocks shallow critical appeals to supposed universal everyday reality and questions assumptions behind familiar evaluative categories.

## Tactical anatomy

**Compositional method:** separate *the work's designed relational pattern* from the temporal sequence in which its parts are drafted. This can permit a segment written late in reading order to be written first in production order.

**Surface craft:** a card may be locally autonomous in its physical container but not independent in the work's final network. The method cannot be modelled as every card doing a single job.

**Reflexive editing:** nonsequential assembly still creates dependencies that require revision when inserted into the whole. This is a modelling hypothesis; inspect manuscript evidence before claiming the exact procedure.

**Criticism of bad writing:** Nabokov's polemic against fake literary profundity is relevant to STENDHAL's unsuccessful E001 library-essay opening. But *poshlost* is culturally loaded and highly subjective; it cannot be operationalised as a neutral binary AI-slop classifier.

**Interview as artefact:** the interview is itself precomposed, arranged and revised by its subject. This complicates reading the method as spontaneous confession.

## Counter-models

- Smith's self-described micro-manager method gives a very different role to sequence.
- Didion's sentence-born fictional knowledge and characters challenges an over-frozen advance plan.
- Dostoevsky's surviving mutable drafts (as examined by Catteau) complicate any assumption that creative mastery requires knowing the complete shape beforehand.

## Small test

For original material, compare 3 constructive conditions: prefigured full-work graph, linear discovery drafting, and hybrid with permitted global re-planning. Trace whether one condition yields *real* nonlocal dependencies, not just claims of artistic coherence.

Do not score one process as artistically superior without defined regime, repeated testing and external readings.
