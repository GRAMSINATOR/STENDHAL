# Parallel research stream 01 — material perception, fidelity, and situated reuse

**Opened:** 2026-10-12. **Status:** parallel research stream, not canon. **Branch:** `rebuild/enactive-greenfield-2026-10`; do not write to `main` or merge it incidentally.

## Why this stream exists

The ongoing STENDHAL greenfield expedition is wide and source-led. This parallel stream allows a second conversation to investigate the same vault without overwriting the other conversation's navigation or developing a competing taxonomy.

**North star is unchanged:** fine-grained **enactive writing craft**, recursively developed with extraction and experimental revision. An author/text/culture has no obligation to fit our analytic interface. Historical source accounts, observations, hypotheses and demonstrated mechanisms are different kinds of object.

The operator's important correction stands: the phonological problem is possible **under-indexing** of sound structure in the generation process (representation, accessibility and/or spontaneous deployment), **not** simply deficient downstream control.

## No premature unification

The three findings expose different research questions and should not become three sliders in a “prose engine”:

| Witness | Locally observed issue | What remains unknown |
|---|---|---|
| Liu Xie | Composer's self-audition versus external sound | Textual-critical readings, historical pronunciation, applicability to neural models |
| Gopen/Swan | Structural reorganisation can imply competing readings | Intended scientific prioritisation, reader populations, controlled outcomes |
| Fox/Meno | Conventional paired lines can be deployed as a situation-specific response | Performance acoustics, live reception, original-language grammatical fine points |

They *suggest* a useful discipline: study separately the actual **material alteration**, the **reader/listener's uptake**, the **writer's conceptual act**, and the **culture/history of the utterance**. This is a *research checklist*, not a fixed four-factor representation of writing.

## Candidate probes — to run, not to claim

### P-A: internal versus external audition

Give participants/model a short source in which multiple sound patterns are plausible. Have them compose without a sound-specific request. Then compare unaided text-based judgments, delayed judgments, and human oral/heard readings. Independently annotate segmental and suprasegmental evidence. Do not treat LLM descriptions of their own hidden “hearing” as introspective data. Interpret results against existing [phonological indexing research](../../research/04-phonological-under-indexing.md) and the [proposed Experiment 005](../../experiments/005-phonological-indexing/README.md).

**Can fail productively:** if perceived improvement is driven by changed semantic associations rather than phonological properties, say so. If output reveals adequate spontaneous phonological patterning, abandon deficit-first assumptions for that regime.

### P-B: multi-valid scientific reconstruction

Offer editors a technically constrained sentence with two live interpretations, withholding author-intent evidence at first. Test whether they can identify the branching point rather than choose the slickest rewrite. Then introduce factual and contextual information. Score preserved claims, added assertions, unresolved ambiguity and reader uptake **separately**.

**Can fail productively:** a very legible output that confidently invents a relation is an epistemic loss, not a win.

### P-C: destructive compression of parallel meaning

Present a paired utterance with a documented situational function. Produce (a) flat propositional summary, (b) repeated-form version, (c) contextual interpretation. Examine what each loses; don't reward superficial alliteration or literal duplication. Never claim to reproduce a Rotenese original after an English paraphrase.

**Can fail productively:** in some regimes, compression really may preserve everything relevant to the actual purpose.

### Observed dimensions of difference—do not turn this into a single classification

- Arabic text: *scope of denial and conversational presuppositions*.
- Prakrit poem: *literal communicative act versus contextually inferred practical uptake*.
- Sanskrit sound material: *phoneme / rasa suitability as a culturally situated aesthetic doctrine*.
- English novel: *source and felt proximity of verbal judgments, without change of narrative person*.
- Expert opposition: *same interpretive endpoint does not establish the same cognitive/explanatory mechanism*, and the endpoint itself can be debated.

These are not commensurate numbers or parallel “layers”. One may interfere with the others at any scale.

**P-E — suggestion vs inference:** Give a speaker's line with progressive context disclosure, collect interpretation, rationale, and uncertainty **without** treating retrospective explanations as evidence of internal model process.

**P-F — consonant-context and voice gradient:** Compare phonology labelling with spontaneous sound-sensitive composition across very different aesthetics; independently study Austen-like narrator/character interpenetration by change-localised rewrites and reader judgments. No cross-language transplant of Sanskrit phoneme taste.

### Gate remaining

## Expedition III — diagnosis-of-diagnosis and semantic collateral (2026-10-12)

**Method:** no “bad style guide” prosecution by default. Hold the exact source passage, its critic's reading and the strongest counter-reading alongside one another, at the level of actual grammatical constructions and compatible interpretations. Distinguish rhetoric, grammar and preservation of thought.

**New source dossiers**:

- [E07-02 — Strunk/White, Pullum and Ferguson](../territories/07-failure-ai/excavations/E07-02-strunk-white-pullum-ferguson-at-the-grammatical-locus.md). Primary 1920 Strunk text and full named critics. Pullum is right that most “before” examples in a passage under “Use the active voice” are not passive; Ferguson counters that the passage may concern **tame constructions broadly**, not assert four passives. Strunk himself explicitly qualifies the passive prohibition. The 1920 text and later edition citations also differ (“verb” vs “transitive”), pending facsimile edition collation.
- [E07-03 — Stronger prose changing the claim](../territories/07-failure-ai/excavations/E07-03-stronger-prose-changes-the-claim-microprobe.md). Original quantitative/qualitative shifts in Strunk's own before/after rewrites: “not much use” becomes “useless”; a sentence with leaves becomes an image of ground coverage; a heard guitar gains personifying mood. Original candidate test packet separates information fidelity, grammatical voice, and preferred expression. **Not run**.
- [PF11 — *Emma* source-variant gate](../territories/02-linguistic-microfabric/excavations/PF11-emma-pronoun-witness-gate.md). Several web transcriptions disagree between “his representation” and “this representation” near Emma's free-indirect self-accusation. Located 1816 three-volume first-edition scans, but **haven't verified the page image**. Do not attribute digital transcription or edition variance to Austen's compositional choices.

### New candidate failure modes, not a global taxonomy

- A grammatical observation is correct but a critic attributes an overstrong claim to the original writer.
- A style intervention successfully intensifies verbal force by **silently altering degrees of certainty, quantity or causation**.
- A plausible micro-reading attributes its effect to an **authorial decision never independently confirmed in a reliable textual witness**.

### Priority test

Run the E07-03 packet with judges blinded to the style manual's headings. First ask only what each sentence commits to; then ask which is better in different explicit situations; only finally reveal the source advice. A discriminating editor should neither identify everything as a passive nor promote the most vigorous rewrite when it falsifies the brief. Measure different kinds of success separately and solicit countercontexts. No fixed metric of good prose has been introduced.

**Caution:** Source criticism of Austen's facsimile remains an open task. Keep literal observed string differences separate from theory of free indirect style and from conjecture about Austen's intentional revisions.

## Operator correction: intention and outcome co-generate without requiring each other (2026-10-12)

**Read [the complete correction and research plan](2026-10-12-02-intention-outcome-autopoiesis-and-partial-control.md) before promoting an intention-centred fidelity criterion.** Outcomes exist and matter even where no intention is recoverable; intention may itself arise recursively from textual outcomes. Skill does not require conscious control of all interacting properties; productive undercontrol can be constitutive of style. Scientific fidelity remains task-appropriate, not a universal court of literary value. None of these statements licenses invented agency or invalid witness attribution. No new mandatory process was adopted.


## Temporary modelling phase — M01 / Expressive Ecology (2026-10-12)

Following the operator's observation that characteristic **looseness** may emerge from uneven awareness/control across intertwined scales, the stream has entered a **temporary-model / break / return-to-research** cadence, not a production architecture lock.

**[M01 — three vulnerable complementary models](../../design/provisional-models/M01-expressive-ecology.md):**
- **A, expressive field:** what overlaps and interacts in the actual material;
- **B, generative trajectory:** how text, process, intention, results and later intentions may relate nonlinearly or remain independent;
- **C, constraint/freedom ecology:** apparent looseness, patterned asymmetry and stylistic texture across different layers, critically separated from claims about the maker's conscious control.

**[EXP-006 — adversarial breakers](../../experiments/006-expressive-ecology-breakers/README.md)** contains eight test designs and one openly self-assessed synthetic dry run (tight/loose/fragmentary/associative variations of one fiction kernel). No human-reader or multi-model experiment is reported as completed.

**Immediate correction:** visible looseness does not identify actual production control. A tightly revised simulation and an unmonitored spontaneous draft can have similar textures. Conversely, highly deliberate local regularity and chance stylistic effects can coexist.

**Proposed rivals:** M02 constraint topology without assumed intention or trajectory, M03 situated reception-first, M04 material-generation-first, M05 adversarial anti-model. Build separately and allow contradictory conclusions; measure explanatory discrimination and failures rather than theory breadth. Preserve the source vault and evidence statuses as independent authority from the model.


## Operator clarification: ubiquitous primitive creativity vs sophistication (2026-10-12)

**The operator's correction to our initial M02 formulation is decisive:** all humans are creative; simple abstractions such as ADD, SUBTRACT, ROTATE and INVERT can be deeply assimilated as root tools and applied almost anywhere, even indiscriminately, with creative consequences. More advanced cognition makes more **sophisticated abstractions** deeply assimilable; it does **not** imply a scalar of more/higher creativity. Do **not** make intelligent choice, optimal transfer, specialist artistry or post-hoc evaluation the criterion for whether a novel possibility was creatively generated.

**Updated and created research:**
- [M02 — corrected abstraction metabolism and opportunity field](../../design/provisional-models/M02-abstraction-metabolism-and-impulse-field.md): explicitly distinguishes elementary generativity, sophistication, craft, effect and selection; preserves the operator's strong claim as a hypothesis to test.
- [Tool opportunity field](../../research/06-tool-opportunity-field.md): elementary abstractions × physical/linguistic/social/time/inferential substrates; conceptual instruments distinguished from software. Practical implementation can begin with tiny traceable transforms and branch records.
- [EXP-007 — primitive transformer cross-product](../../experiments/007-primitive-transformers/README.md): blind testing of ADD/SUBTRACT/ROTATE/INVERT across multiple targets, with novelty assessed separately from quality and complexity. **Not run**.
- [EXP-008 — multi-agent brushstroke relay](../../experiments/008-brushstroke-relay/README.md): incomplete sentence/clause handoffs, branch-preserving local impulses, equal-budget comparisons against single-author work; includes explicitly synthetic illustration. **No true multi-agent run yet**.

The proposed operator field need not be a weighted numeric vector space or hand-curated taxonomy; **the easiest test is simply to apply the most elementary abstractions to unexpected targets and observe what becomes possible.** Beware retroactive operator explanations and easy stylistic gimmicks. The collaborative agent vision remains a separate potentially fruitful regime, not yet product architecture.

## Primitive inversion as reciprocal attribution — “leaves blowing / wind rustling” (2026-10-12)

The operator identified a regularly used microscopic writing move: **invert qualifiers/predications between connected participants**. Ordinary associations (wind blows, leaves rustle) may remain inferable while the wording crosses them (“leaves were blowing / wind was rustling”), sometimes producing layered information in exceptionally little language. The claim is conditional: no literal 3x metric, guaranteed poetic improvement or necessary historical explanation from one named figure.

- [Research 07: reciprocal predicates, historical hypallage parallels and information stacking](../../research/07-reciprocal-predicate-transfer-and-information-stacking.md).
- [EXP-009: reciprocal attribution probes with negative controls](../../experiments/009-reciprocal-attribution/README.md).
- [M02 amended](../../design/provisional-models/M02-abstraction-metabolism-and-impulse-field.md) to treat relational property/agency assignment as a primitive operation target.

A useful invented pair for scrutiny: “Her hands raced over the keys. The piano trembled beneath them” → “Her hands trembled over the keys. The piano raced beneath them.” Its new reading may be rich precisely because **it changes the apparent experience and agency**; it cannot be described as simply preserving facts. Opposite semantic no-ops and destructive transfers should be actively sought.

## M03 — Process as the missing coupling of M01 and M02 (2026-10-12)

The operator now makes the central requirement explicit: **some generative intention → write/act → exquisite perception of actual output → reaction and further creative transformations → refactoring/modification of intention → more work**, recursively, with optional branches, abandonment, or immediate success. Intentions and outcomes may influence each other or exist independently, and the loop does not dictate conscious human cognition.

The process may begin in ordered thought, partial text, chaos, deliberate redundancy or surprise. Redundancy may be produced to collapse/fuse/shake later **or kept intact for intended obsessive rhythm**. A temporarily rough draft can be useful for its future affordances. Fine perception must see surface traces, cross-scale interactions, missing literal facts, context-support, speculative reader effects, and preservation-worthy accidents—without manufacturing meanings.

- [M03 — Transformative Composition Process](../../design/provisional-models/M03-transformative-composition-process.md) now foregrounds an explicit generative intention–enaction–perception–reaction–revised intention kernel, while permitting recursive and nonlinear application.
- [EXP-012 — Multilayer Process Studio](../../experiments/012-multilayer-process-studio/README.md) documents four distinct *researcher-authored*, unvalidated source-specific routes on the cold pianist seed: redundant surplus→fusion→context; chaos→selective structure; order→fracture; intentional redundancy retained. Includes exact observational/semantic consequences and a real experimental comparison plan.
- [Forensic perception specification](../../design/03-forensic-perception.md) gains an actionable perceptual-return protocol and failure criteria.

The first cycle's important example is that coldness can migrate into moving hands while a heart's beat migrates onto the piano, but literal claims may be lost; an additional musical-rest scene can emerge as a **new intention**, not an original requirement. Reader uptake remains unmeasured; do not report literary improvements as demonstrated outcomes.

No automatic replacement of M01/M02; M03 provides a *testing substrate* for how they might interact. Retain contradictory routes and fail fast on fake subtlety or contrived explanations.

## EXP-013 — First executable process protocol, movement versus flat rhetorical redundancy (2026-10-12)

The operator identified a separate structural failure common in AI prose: overuse of rhetorical devices and repeated slight paraphrases can destroy momentum and dilute meaning, even while remaining smooth. A work must be able to move through **information delivery, setup, expression, timing, building pressure, variation and release**—not always by asserting a fresh factual proposition. Effective repetition can be a movement; mere lexical variety can be a stasis.

[EXP-013 — Directional Process Studio](../../experiments/013-directional-process-prototype/README.md) documents the first **executed** P0 deterministic process harness: 2 genres (pianist fiction, nonfiction transit), 10 researcher-authored variants, 13 trace events per run, and 16 passing local unittest cases in the expanded session package. A [portable Python microversion](../../experiments/013-directional-process-prototype/mini.py) also lives in GitHub. The tested expanded downloadable code bundle was generated in the same conversation; don't claim the repo's compact version and session bundle are identical test environments.

Results: the 28-word thin bus report has **one** distinct *annotated* contribution and four flat restatements; a 33-word made-up paradox relies on an unestablished expected covariance; a rhetorical passenger refrain makes **two unsupported claims** in nonfiction; the pianist's repeated `cold` can either be exploratory surplus or progress through a deliberate turn to warmth. A genuinely executed predicate-swapping function revealed grammatical/attachment failures that a more structured core-transfer repaired.

**Scope:** annotations and candidate prose were hand-authored; measured movement is only a contribution-tag diagnostic, **not** empirical information density, literary value or autonomous LLM criticism. A next P1 needs actual model proposals, independent perceptual returns, dynamic intention revision, actual reader response, and equal-compute controls. The Five Faces remain an optional quick lens check, not merged into the process model.

## Post-execution adversary — Giono, valuable absurdity and real choice (2026-10-12)

Operator observation: the first P0 machine swap “The piano raced over the keys” was described as invalid because actor/spatial complements no longer described ordinary realistic geometry. But **it could be extraordinarily effective comedy, absurdism, psychological displacement or a high-voltage tonal rupture**. Validity depends on the task; literal impossibility is not a universal literary defect. Even a serious work's sudden absurdity may create a deeply productive contradiction. The user's proposed standard is **real consequences and real choices**, including anti-synergistic vectors and tonal desynchronies that may themselves serve a larger structure.

Jean Giono's *Un roi sans divertissement* provides a genuine source-based adversary: the final cigar/dynamite substitution, visual explosion, comic-cosmic head image and Pascal question are grounded in a cold, burlesque, brutal work with long-range anticipation and epistemically limited narration. Full final paragraph accessed as educational / publisher excerpt, while larger-plot context came from secondary close reading; **not** a claim the whole book was independently read here.

## M04/M05 — Scoped tonal worlds inside a reconfigurable strategy topology (2026-10-12)

The operator introduced a new *layered* view: **radical constraints** can give a work a strong identifiable character, but they apply in different domains and scopes. A scientific spacefaring story can have strict physical causality, expressionist emotional language and a single absurdly voiced character. Breaking a local expectation may be productive even when a different layer remains inviolable.

Then came a crucial architectural correction: **the deeper system is not a giant tonal vector**. There are competing views and approaches; we need reconfigurable nested decision trees/graphs whose **questions and tools are themselves reusable, relocatable, replaceable and evolvable**. Suggested nodes ask whether to impose radical constraints, whether to colour style strongly, at which scale, and what to do when the work resists its original constraints. Question ordering has conditional dependencies, not one universal aesthetic workflow. A future creative/perceptual cycle may change the harness itself.

## Temporal mixture and the next real perceptual experiment (2026-10-12)

The operator observes that writing is **a cocktail evolving over time**: tones, registers, constraints, source/character presence, purposes and expressive devices interact, fade and change significance. A long text may open room for more shifts but need not use them; a short paragraph may remain tonally steady or hinge on one extremely consequential contrast. *Variation itself is not progress.*

The immediate work should **not** be M06 or another hand-scored tone matrix. [EXP-016](../../experiments/016-temporal-perception-p1/README.md) proposes real P1 comparisons: a short paragraph, first-person article with a remembered scene and quotation, and a literary scene with strictly physical causation but flexible expression. Compare one-shot vs “polish” vs a blind separate *perceptual return → changed intention → rewriting* process at known budgets. Require witness-grounded observations of actual local and nonlocal movement; allow stillness and unedited successes.

**State:** P1 experiment specified in GitHub, not independently run; a model execution interface and separate contexts would be required to support autonomous/perceptual claims.
