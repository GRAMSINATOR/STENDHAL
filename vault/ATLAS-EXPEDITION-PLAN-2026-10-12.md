# STENDHAL — Magellan atlas: eight distinct research regions

**Date:** 2026-10-12. **Status:** expedition planning and source reconnaissance, not a unified ontology of writing.

## Governing objective

Acquire radically diverse, independently grounded perceptions of writing and expression, including their fine-grained tactical fabric, without prematurely deriving a universal technique model. The exact material of prose, including **unremarkable, restrained, scientific and functional prose**, has priority over grand narrative interpretation.

**Eight regions** (search territories, not mutually exclusive categories):

1. **Genetic criticism / actual revision**: Flaubert, Proust, Dickinson, Joyce, Beckett, Woolf, manuscript witnesses; distinguish actual chronological change from folio ordering. Initial GR01–GR04 exist. New source: Geert Lernout, 'From Varieties of Genetic Experience to Radical Philology' (2023), https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/genetic-criticism-and-the-creative-process/from-varieties-of-genetic-experience-to-radical-philology/E614294BD4AFD1B62C2DE7D66E072947 . Lernout reports disciplinary criticism of genetic criticism itself: valuable challenge to overvaluing manuscript history.
2. **Great practitioners in fine-grained disagreement**: Stein, Davis, Le Guin, Nabokov, Hemingway, Woolf, Baldwin, Morrison, Bukowski, and others. Source actual craft statements and the *same concrete marks*, not paraphrased opposing philosophies. Preserve agreements and disputes, including distinctions in medium and context.
3. **Phonology, phonetics, syntax and word-internal material**: Jakobson, Tsur, Fónagy, linguists, speech science, computational probes. New Chinese-language countercheck: Yue, Shen and Lu, 'Phun-Bench' (ACL 2026), https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1041/ . Models often recall pronunciations yet struggle to use sound knowledge flexibly. English-only phonological claims must not generalise across writing systems. Also Loakman et al. (2024), sound symbolism and multimodal LLMs, https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.167/ .
4. **Oratory, rhetoric and speechwriting as performance**: Quintilian, Cicero, modern speechwriters, courtroom advocates, theatre. Focus sentence stress, breath, audience and timing rather than argumentative architecture.
5. **Scientific and explanatory prose**: Gopen/Swan, Joseph Williams, Helen Sword, disciplinary norms, scientific rhetoric and negative cases; technical precision and epistemic qualifications as craft, not ornamental prose.
6. **Accomplished criticism and bad writing**: accomplished critics evaluating acclaimed writers, sometimes unjustly; exact locus, quoted objection, counter-reading, eventual reception. Bad writing is a research object, including intentionally flat or ugly expression.
7. **Empirical reader and writer cognition**: eye tracking, auditory imagery, process tracing, revision, expertise. Magyari et al. (2020) combined eye tracking and questionnaire reports to study mental imagery in literary reading, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33828798/ . Reader effects are not interchangeable with critic intuition.
8. **Computational stylistics and generative systems**: corpus statistics, stylometry, acoustic and lexical probes, generative creativity, NLP models and failure modes. Liao & Shi (2026) phonological encoding https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.634/ ; Haslett et al. (2026) find subword spelling may encode some functional/taxonomic information missing from distributional signals, https://aclanthology.org/2026.tacl-1.113/ . Thus not all word-internal tokenisation effects are negative.

## Expedition protocol for each region

- Start with **5–12 unlike primary sources** rather than several followers of one school; annotate date, medium, language, edition, access and source status.
- Seek at least **two genuinely qualified differences** within the region. 'Qualified' means distinguished practitioners/scholars, not high-level abstractions. A disagreement about a comma is ideal.
- For each claim, record **exact source locus**, direct observable trace, the author's actual assertion, independent observation, and what remains uncertain.
- Include an **adversarial/negative case**: criticism of a successful writer, failures of an accepted method, or a case where a celebrated device harms the writing.
- Prefer micro-fabric evidence: lexical choice, syntax, punctuation, phonology, clause attachment, cadence, nonlocal echo. Allow macro relevance only when grounded in the words.
- Do not infer technique from every feature or invent a deterministic generation operator from a scholar's metaphor.
- Mark **primary-read / abstract-read / lead-only / unverified** separately.
- Collect counterexamples across genres and languages before attempting invariance.

## Priority sequence

**A. Next excavation:** genetic variants (already under way), but broaden to Barthes's moving fragments and a critic questioning genetic criticism's explanatory reach.

**B. Parallel frontier:** phonological under-indexing, including Chinese sound-based tasks, English sound symbolism, and contradictory findings about subword information.

**C. Then:** practitioners disagreeing over sentence punctuation and diction, scientific plain prose, and critic-on-critic conflicts; follow with reader cognition, speechwriting, computational styles.

## Operational quality gate

Each new source must yield either (a) a directly observable new distinction, (b) an independently documented conflict, (c) a negative counterexample, or (d) a methodologically important limitation. Merely adding a bibliography item does not constitute an excavation.

## Scope restraint

No fixed number of techniques per sentence, no single vector model, no mandatory 6–10 passes, no claim that aesthetic effect is measurable from spelling alone, and no claim that authorial manuscript corrections reveal motivation without interpretation.
